# Q-011 The confidence scale and its words

## What is unknown

On what scale does a Method express confidence, and in what words is it shown to a Reader ([[CTX-06]], [[ASM-05]], [[OUT-10]])? The words are ruled in principle (for example "high confidence"); the scale, and the mapping from it to words, are not. The floor below which a scope decision is `UNKNOWN` ([[REL-02]]) is set by the scope Method on this scale.

## Why this priority

P1: every Method records a confidence ([[EVD-02]]) and the first Methods are about to be designed. They can proceed under the fallback below, so it is not P0; it is not P2, because Readers would see words that no calibration supports.

## Answered when

The scale and its bands of words are written, and on a labelled set of at least 200 Judgements the observed accuracy within each band lies within 10 percentage points of the confidence that band states.

## If it stays unknown

Confidence is a number from 0 to 1. A Reader sees "low confidence" below 0.5, "moderate confidence" from 0.5 to 0.8, and "high confidence" above 0.8.
