# Q-026 How a source becomes Article Records that meet ACQ-02

## What is unknown

How does a source that the owner or a Curator adds become Article Records that hold the item's text with every structure class the page shows, and that meet [[ACQ-02]], [[ACQ-10]] and [[ACQ-13]] as they now read: its title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time, each held in one Field state; where the item credits as its author its Item publisher, or its source recorded in place of it, that it does, that credit never among its Authors; and any value BEACON supplies recorded as a Supplied value with the procedure that supplied it and that procedure's version? This supersedes [[Q-019]], which asked which tier, in which order, each metadata field is taken from. The experiments on that question, [[X-002]] to [[X-006]], raised parts that no order of tiers settles, and each is now its own question:
- how a source's link becomes a checked page-structure configuration ([[Q-020]]);
- which organisations listed jointly inside a body are Authors, and how such a list is read ([[Q-021]]);
- what makes a publication time reproducible and corroborated ([[Q-022]]);
- how later Stages use where each value was found, or that it was supplied ([[Q-023]]);
- whether a targeted model call may decide a field ([[Q-024]]);
- who may correct a field, and what Readers see of a supplied one ([[Q-025]]).

What stays here is the whole: one acquisition design, from a source to its records, that those parts fit into, and a measurement of it on items and sources no design choice has seen.

## Why this priority

P1: the Extractor is designed on the answer, and that design is the next collection work. The work can proceed meanwhile under the fallback below, so it is not P0. It is not P2, because until it is answered every record leaves empty the undeclared fields the owner's hand check found missing on [[X-001]]'s pages, and no extraction design has been approved to fill them.

## Answered when

The owner approves an acquisition design that names, for a source configured by the step [[Q-020]] asks for: how its items are fetched and their text extracted; and for each of title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time, where the value is taken from, what is recorded where the source's values disagree, and what is supplied where the source gives none. And one experiment, frozen before its labels exist, on items no design choice has seen, published in the twelve months before the run, from each source as far back as its archive reaches, from at least one source that no earlier experiment configured as well as sources that were, reports against the owner's hand labels:
- the rate at which each field is right (titles alike after case and whitespace are set aside, a site name the kept title carries counting as a miss; Item publishers alike after case and whitespace are set aside; Author sets alike in any order and case, a name listed twice counting as a miss, with beside it the rate when Unicode normal form and runs of whitespace are also set aside; dates on the same calendar day), for each set of places the kept value was found in (two or more that agree, and one, by which place), for Supplied values apart, by the procedure that supplied them, and for fields held with no value apart, with its count, and with the rates also holding on the most recent six months, reported apart;
- the rate at which the text keeps each structure class, on that experiment's sample, with the share the owner confirmed by eye;
- for the source no earlier experiment configured, the same rates apart;
- that every record the experiment produced holds what [[ACQ-02]] requires of it.

[[Q-020]] and [[Q-022]] ask for measurements of their own; one experiment may serve more than one of these questions, and this one decides none of theirs.

## If it stays unknown

Records keep [[X-001]]'s region extractor for text. Authors and publication times are taken as the fallbacks of [[Q-021]] and [[Q-022]] state. A title is taken from what the page declares or its listing gives, and supplied where the source gives none. The Item publisher is the one the item names, or the item's source. Each field is held in one Field state ([[ACQ-10]]): where each value the source states was found, with every other value where they disagree; that the source states none; or no kept value with its reason; and a Supplied value with the procedure that supplied it and that procedure's version ([[ACQ-13]]).

## Evidence so far

[[Q-019]] holds the per-tier tables and the full accounts; this is their summary. Q-019 was open when this superseded it.

- [[X-001]] kept every raw page its sources listed. Where a page declared no author or date its stack took a generic extractor's guess and labelled it as the page's.
- [[X-002]] measured every tier on 30 labelled items and suggested an order per field, in sample.
- [[X-003]] froze that order and tested it on 60 held-out items over six months: not met, all three fields right on 44 of 60. Twelve of the 16 misses were authors on two sources, where the owner labelled a credit no tier found. The text extractor kept at least 90 per cent of every structure class on 211 of 214 HTML items by script, but the owner confirmed only 9 of 20 by eye.
- [[X-004]] tested mechanisms for those misses on 60 held-out labels over twelve months: not met on metadata, 50 of 60 (54 needed). Structure met its bar: the owner confirmed all 18 verifiable items. Raw pages were kept for 387 of 389. Four of the ten misses were a model not applying credit rules the owner had settled.
- [[X-005]] stated those rules to the model and re-tested on the same 60 labels, so not held out: not met, authors right on 54 of 60 (56 needed). Its model copied names well and judged their role badly, giving a false credit on 7 of 27 pages that credit no one.
- [[X-006]], which supersedes X-005, decided each field from deterministic sources that must not contradict, with the site's own organisation read as the publisher and a byline selector per source, and no model call: met, 57 of 60, on the third pass over the same 60 items and on labels reread under the publisher rule. It reports no per-tier rate against the hand labels, and claims nothing for unseen pages or for a source nobody configured. The owner called it "a code implementation guidance, not necessarily a design to keep" (2026-10-01).

What no experiment has measured: any design on a source nobody configured; the deterministic design on held-out items; a model call as the decider of a disputed or empty field; and the same page's metadata derived on two different days. [[CP-001]] changed [[ACQ-02]] after X-006 ran, so no experiment's records were built to the row as it now reads.
