# Q-013 Item identity within a source

## What is unknown

When are two items that Collections of one source return the same item, and on what evidence (the URL, the source's own identifier, the title, the text), so that BEACON can tell an Encounter and a Revision from a new item?

## Why this priority

P1: the collection component is not yet entered, and the fallback is workable. Once it is entered, this becomes P0: the store's item key cannot be settled without it.

## Answered when

On a sample of at least one month of Collections from the initial Source Set, the criterion's same-or-different decisions agree with a labeller's on at least 98 per cent of item pairs, with the false-"same" rate reported separately.

## If it stays unknown

The same source and the same URL after normalisation mean the same item; anything else is a new item. Duplicates that this admits join the same development ([[DEV-06]]).

## Evidence so far

[[X-001]] and [[X-002]] met one item under several addresses within a single source: tracking parameters (`utm_*`, `ref`, and a `?ai-allowed=1` that one sitemap adds to every URL), a trailing slash that a listing's identifiers lack and the page's own address carries, redirects that add a slash, and one paper under an abstract, an HTML and a PDF address and a version suffix. Canonicalising the address (dropping tracking parameters and fragments) kept the same items recurring across X-001's seven runs of one day. Neither experiment tested a Revision, or identity across days.
