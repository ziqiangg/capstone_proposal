# Q-007 The initial subject vocabulary

## What is unknown

Which values should the subject vocabulary start with, and at what granularity?

## Why this priority

P2: the vocabulary grows through BEACON's proposals, which a Curator accepts ([[CLS-05]]), and a Curator may add values directly, so starting small costs nothing yet; no chunk is tagged before the classification work starts.

## Answered when

A list of values, each with a one-line definition, applied to a sample of at least 100 chunks leaves no more than 10 per cent of them with no value that applies.

## If it stays unknown

The subject vocabulary starts empty and grows through proposals a Curator accepts ([[CLS-05]]).
