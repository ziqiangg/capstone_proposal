# Q-018 Privacy obligations per class of personal data

## What is unknown

Which obligations of Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act apply to each class of personal data BEACON holds or sends, and what does each require of BEACON? The classes: user accounts ([[GOV-11]]); linked chat accounts ([[DLV-18]], [[DLV-26]]); consent records ([[DLV-13]]); per-recipient Delivery outcomes ([[DLV-12]]); attribution in audit and correction records ([[GOV-16]], [[FBK-02]]); and persons named in articles ([[DEV-10]]). It includes the transfer of recipients' chat identifiers and of Delivery text to the delivery channel's operator, which stores cloud chats in several jurisdictions and does not encrypt them end to end, and of item text to the model provider.

Persons named in articles are held in more places than the record's Authors: an Item publisher who is a person, as a one-person site's is, with the record of an item crediting it ([[ACQ-02]]); raw pages, kept however old the item and wherever stored, which hold most names (quoted people, commenters) until an erasure removes them ([[ACQ-18]]); Source Set entries, in a name an entry holds as the site's own, a one-person site's source name, and parts first written by hand, proposed to an Onboarding; per-item experiment reports; the values as delivered, where a field fails the rows [[ACQ-12]] names, kept apart from the record's fields and removed by an erasure ([[ACQ-12]], [[ACQ-18]]); and a digest of an erased item's text, where one is kept so that the item is still recognised as held. Each is within this question's scope.

## Why this priority

P1: chat identifiers and Delivery text leave Singapore with the first Delivery, and the retention defaults ([[PERSONAL_DATA_RETENTION]], [[CONSENT_RECORD_RETENTION]]) need a legal check. The work can start under the fallback, so it is not P0 until the delivery component is entered; it is not P2, because the region and erasure design are expensive to change once built.

## Answered when

A written view from counsel or the organisation's legal function states, for each class above, the purpose, the retention limit, the protection required and whether the transfer out of Singapore is permitted and on what conditions; it confirms whether the purpose of attribution justifies keeping the names of persons named in articles for the life of the record under the Act's retention limit; and the owner accepts it.

## If it stays unknown

Every store is in the Singapore region; only chat identifiers and Delivery text leave it, to the delivery channel's operator, and item text to the model provider; a former recipient's linked chat account and Delivery outcomes, from when the Reader stops being a recipient, and a deactivated user's contact details, from deactivation, are erased after [[PERSONAL_DATA_RETENTION]], and consent records after [[CONSENT_RECORD_RETENTION]] ([[DLV-31]], [[DLV-32]], [[GOV-19]]); names of persons in articles are kept with the record, and in its raw page until an erasure removes it ([[ACQ-02]], [[ACQ-18]]).

## Evidence so far

Records kept by [[X-001]] and [[X-002]] hold the names of persons credited in articles (bylines, paper authors). X-002's metadata model ran on the local machine, so that step sent no item text to a model provider; whether every Method can run locally is not shown.
