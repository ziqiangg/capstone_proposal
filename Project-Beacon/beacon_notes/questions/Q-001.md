# Q-001 Whether a mapping's evidence supports it

## What is unknown

How should BEACON decide whether a mapping's cited evidence supports the relationship it asserts, and what should happen to a mapping that is not supported?

## Why this priority

P1: Readers act on mappings, so an unsupported one is a defensibility failure, and the mapping work is about to start. It can proceed under the assumption below, so it is not P0; it is not P2, because the assumption publishes every mapping, the unsupported ones included, as soon as Readers see output. [[CTX-08]] waits on this answer.

## Answered when

A stated support criterion, applied to a labelled set of at least 100 mappings, agrees with the labellers' supported or unsupported judgement on at least 90 per cent of them, with the rate at which unsupported mappings are judged supported reported separately; and the owner has ruled what happens to an unsupported mapping.

## If it stays unknown

Every mapping is published with its Evidence locators and its explanation ([[CTX-06]]), and none is withheld; a Curator corrects a wrong mapping through [[FBK-01]].
