# Q-021 Organisations credited jointly inside an item's body

## What is unknown

When a page names, inside its body rather than in a byline or its declared metadata, the organisations that jointly released it, as a joint advisory does, what does BEACON record as the item's authors ([[ACQ-02]])? This is first a rule and then a mechanism. The owner ruled on 2026-10-01 that agencies a page lists as jointly releasing the item stay authors, with the site's own agency among them recorded as the publisher; but the labels of two sets read lists of this kind differently, so which lists the ruling covers is not settled. If such lists are authors, how is a list read from the body by a mechanism that is not a rule for a named site, given that no byline selector reaches it?

## Why this priority

P2: it touches one of [[X-006]]'s 60 test items, and the fallback records that item's authors as empty, not as someone wrong. It is not P1, because the Extractor's design met its bar without it and no work about to start turns on it.

## Answered when

The owner rules which lists inside a body are an item's authors, and every item of that kind in the labelled sets of [[X-002]], [[X-003]] and [[X-004]] is labelled to that ruling; and, if such lists are authors, a mechanism reads, on at least 10 items of that kind not used to build it, every listed organisation and nothing else on at least 9, and adds a list on none of at least 30 labelled items that carry no such list.

## If it stays unknown

Authors come only from declared metadata and the byline element. A list inside a body is not read, and an item whose only credit is such a list has empty authors, its status recording that no source gave a value.

## Evidence so far

On [[X-006]]'s run, test S086 names 26 jointly releasing organisations in its body; under the publisher rule its reread label keeps 25 and removes the site's own agency. No selector reaches the list, so it is one of the run's three misses and the only one on a kept page. Check S059, a page of the same kind, is labelled "no one credited". [[X-005]]'s first design, with a model reading the credit text, also missed S086. The owner ruled on 2026-09-30 that one test item never justifies a rule, and recorded the list as a known limit.
