# Q-024 Whether a targeted model call may decide a metadata field

## What is unknown

May a model call targeted at one field ever decide an item's title, authors or publication time, where the values the source states for it disagree or it states none, and on what measured evidence? [[X-006]]'s design keeps two such calls. Where the stated values disagree, the call is recorded and changes nothing, and the first voter in order keeps its value. A last-resort call may, by the owner's ruling, supply a title or date the source states none of, never authors; it was off in X-006's run and has never been measured as a decider. So two things are open: whether the call where stated values disagree may ever decide, and whether the last resort's deciding holds when measured. [[Q-026]] asks for one approved acquisition design, from a source to its records; it can be answered by a design in which no model decides, which leaves this open.

## Why this priority

P2: on [[X-006]]'s 58 kept test pages one field was disputed and none needed the last resort, and on development and check the first voter in order was right on every disagreement, so the fallback has cost one development date (S210) and nothing on the test items. It is not P1, because no work about to start needs a model to decide; it would rise if a new source makes common the fields whose stated values disagree, or the titles and publication times the source states none of. Nothing in BEACON's first build measures it, since the model call it concerns is deferred.

## Answered when

On at least 50 fields whose stated values disagree, or that are a title or publication time the source states none of, drawn from items no design choice has seen and from at least one source not configured during development, the call's value is right at least as often as the value the deterministic design records on the same fields, and the call gives the same answer when its candidates are reordered on at least 95 per cent of them; reported per field, with fields whose values disagree and fields the source states none of apart, and with the count of fields where the call answers "none".

## If it stays unknown

Where the values the source states for a field disagree, the first voter in order keeps its value and any call is recorded only. A title or date the source states none of may be supplied by the last-resort call, as the owner ruled, as a Supplied value with the procedure that supplied it, that procedure's version, and the model's identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings ([[ACQ-13]]) and unmeasured; Authors the source states none of are left unsupplied.

## Evidence so far

On all 89 disagreements among sources on X-005's 90 development and check items, weak sources counted, the first voter in order was right every time; the targeted call was right on 69 and changed its answer under a reorder of the candidates on 14. In [[X-005]]'s first design, where a model read the credit text for every item, it gave a false credit on 7 of 27 pages that credit no one, and about one answer in seven moved when the same content was presented differently. One development date (S210) that only a model read is lost to the deterministic design. Disputes are rare (1 of 174 fields on X-006's kept test pages), so the 50 fields this question needs will take many more items than one labelled set.
