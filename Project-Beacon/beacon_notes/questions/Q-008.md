# Q-008 The assessment criteria and the Significance scale

## What is unknown

Against which criteria should BEACON assess a development's Significance, and on what scale is Significance expressed?

## Why this priority

P1: selection ranks by Significance, and [[MIN_SIGNIFICANCE]] and [[ASSESSMENT_TOLERANCE]] have no value until the scale exists. The assessment work can proceed under the fallback below, so it is not P0; it is not P2, because without a floor every assessed development qualifies for selection.

## Answered when

The criteria and the scale are written, and two assessors applying them independently to at least 50 developments give the same Significance, or values one step apart, for at least 80 per cent of them.

## If it stays unknown

Significance is an ordinal from 1 (low) to 5 (critical), judged on severity, breadth and novelty; [[MIN_SIGNIFICANCE]] and [[ASSESSMENT_TOLERANCE]] stay unset, so every assessed development qualifies and the ranking bound alone ([[SEL-01]]) limits the Recommendation Set.
