# CP-008 COLLECTION_TIMEOUT and completing without error

## The change

Five replacements: the Collection line of [[A-terms]] A2, replaced by itself and one new line after it, Completed without error; [[SRC-04]]'s rationale; the `default` line of [[COLLECTION_TIMEOUT]]; [[SRC-21]]'s requirement sentence and rationale; and the Drift flag line of [[A-terms]]. Every other line of [[A-terms]], [[SRC-04]], [[COLLECTION_TIMEOUT]] and [[SRC-21]] is unchanged, [[SRC-04]]'s frontmatter, requirement sentence and **Verification** line, [[COLLECTION_TIMEOUT]]'s other frontmatter lines and body, and [[SRC-21]]'s frontmatter, heading and **Verification** line included.

**1. [[A-terms]] A2, the Collection line, as it stands:**

```
| Collection | BEACON fetching what one source's Source Set entry returns. |
```

**Replaced by** (two lines, in this order):

```
| Collection | BEACON fetching what one source's Source Set entry returns. |
| Completed without error | Said of a Collection, not ended under OPS-15, that read its source's listing to its end and either fetched the content of at least one listed item BEACON did not already hold or found every listed item already held, a listing with no entries included (SRC-04). A listed item BEACON no longer tries to fetch (ACQ-39) is left out. A page that shows none of what the source's Source Set entry holds as appearing on every page of the source, where it holds any, counts for this term as no fetch, and a listing, or a page of it, as read only where a page answered for it, retries included, shows what the Entry part locating the listing holds. A Collection that ends other than under OPS-15 has completed, in OPS-15's and OPS-16's sense. |
```

**2. [[SRC-04]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** In the output, a dead source looks the same as a quiet week. Only the product can tell the two apart.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
**Rationale.** In the output, a dead source looks the same as a quiet week. Only the product can tell the two apart. A Collection that fetched something new, or had nothing new to fetch, read a working source; one that timed out, did not read its listing to its end or fetched none of the new items it listed did not (A-terms, Completed without error). A bot block's page counts as no fetch where the entry holds something as appearing on every page and the page shows none of it, and leaves the listing unread where no page answered for it, retries included, shows what the entry holds for locating it. An item ACQ-39 stops is left out, lest it freeze a working source's time. An empty listing records the time; SRC-21 flags one new for its source, and a listing whose first page is answered only with pages that show nothing the entry holds for locating it, as a bot wall's are, not one unreached or partly read.
```

**3. [[COLLECTION_TIMEOUT]], the `default` line, as it stands:**

```
default: "15 minutes"
```

**Replaced by:**

```
default: "60 minutes"
```

**4. [[SRC-21]], the requirement sentence and the rationale, as they stand:**

After a keep, how often the source's Collections meet the thing is judged as a rate (SRC-16; A-terms, Drift window). ## Why

The owner's rulings on the [[Q-019]] forms (2026-10-04), the trim ruling of the same day, the
owner's answer of 2026-10-06 on where SRC-04's phrase goes and the owner's answers of the same
day on this draft's seven choices, on a case its revision raised and on its first assessment are
the evidence. They do not answer [[Q-019]]; they define a phrase [[SRC-04]] uses, set a
parameter's starting value and add a whole-Collection event to [[SRC-21]], which no row,
annex line or parameter note as it stands does. This is the seventh proposal of the chain the
trim ruling names (its item 19, "COLLECTION_TIMEOUT"), after [[CP-002]] to [[CP-007]].

**The timeout (F3-11).** F3-11 chose "(b) The Operator raises the parameter for all sources",
with the note "The Operator sets COLLECTION_TIMEOUT to 60 minutes as a starting value. The limit
exists to stop an unreachable source holding up the briefing (OPS-15), not to cut off a slow
source that is working." Item 3 changes the default and nothing else. The bounds still read
true: they say what the parameter limits and that it is "more than 0, and shorter than the time
from 00:00 to CYCLE_DEADLINE", and 60 minutes is within them at [[CYCLE_DEADLINE]]'s default of
08:00. [[OPS-15]]'s rationale ("An unreachable source must not hold the day's Briefing") is
F3-11's reason already. The owner stays the Operator. The per-source limit, the digest's
daily new-article count and the Operator's later raise are outside this change (Out of scope).

**The definition (F3-10, amended).** The trim ruling's "F3-10, amended" reads: "a Collection
completed without error when its listing was read to its end and either at least one fetch
succeeded or every listed article was already held. A source whose new articles cannot be
fetched does not complete." It replaces the spot-check wording of the F3-10 answer, the spot
check being dropped (trim item 4, "F3-09: no spot re-download; held items fetched again only
on a listing signal"). F3-10's note adds that a Collection ended by the timeout has not
completed ("read to the end before COLLECTION_TIMEOUT"), and the owner's answer of 2026-10-06
repeats it ("F3-10 also says a Collection ended by the timeout hasn't completed"). The new line
carries all three; how the first two sentences are reconciled is the owner's ruling 1 below.

**Where it goes (the owner's answer of 2026-10-06).** "An A-terms line if the phrase is used in
more than one row (SRC-04, and SRC-12 if it reads it), so there's one definition", and "SRC-04
itself if only SRC-04 uses it." A search of `requirements` for "completed without error" finds it in
[[SRC-04]] and in the Health entry line of [[A-terms]], which reports "whether its Collection
completed without error (SRC-04)". [[SRC-12]] does not use it. Two places use the phrase, so it
is an A-terms line, placed after the Collection line it qualifies. Its reason goes in
[[SRC-04]]'s rationale, the row that reads it, so the line stays a definition. The case reasoning
the line leaves out (an end under OPS-15 "whatever it read or fetched before"; "whether or not
without error" for any other ended Collection) is in the cases below.

**The empty listing.** The trim ruling's third sentence ("A listing with zero entries, on a
source onboarding never saw empty, is a D2 event on the listing part") is already carried by
[[SRC-21]] and the Drift flag line, which [[CP-006]] applied; this change does not repeat it.
The owner's answer on CP-006's third assessment has "an empty listing still counts as completed
under F3-10". A listing with no entries lists no item BEACON does not hold, so the new line's
second branch holds of it, its page still showing the listing's structure and so read, and the line says so ("a listing with no entries included") so that
it has one outcome: completed without error, whether or not [[SRC-21]] also raises a flag.

**Not a listing read (the owner's answer of 2026-10-06).** A listing answered with a challenge or
error page: "Not a listing read. A listing counts as read only where the page shows what the
source's entry holds for locating its listing (the onboarding 'locating the listing' part: the
feed's format, or the listing's place on the page). A challenge page shows neither, so the listing
was not read and the Collection does not complete. Uses an existing Entry part, no new rule." The
owner's reasons: "It's F3-04's rule applied to the listing. [...] Treating it as an empty listing
would let a blocked source count as completed, which turns SRC-04's 'dead or quiet' signal upside
down"; the other option "relies on SRC-21 to flag the empty listing, but only where the listing was
never empty before. A source whose listing is sometimes legitimately empty would never be flagged,
so a bot wall in front of its listing would look like a quiet week indefinitely"; "Onboarding
already establishes how to locate a source's listing [...] Checking that the answered page shows it
is the obvious test of 'the listing was read', the listing-level twin of F3-04's template check";
and "A genuinely empty listing still works. A real empty page still shows the listing's structure
(an empty feed, an empty listing area), so it's read and is judged as an empty listing as before."
The line names the part in the vault's words, "the Entry part locating the listing" ([[A-terms]],
Onboarding outcome and Tried). It needs no "where it holds any": that part is required, an
Onboarding outcome being a refusal where it is not established, and a source enters the Source Set
only by a Curator's acceptance of an outcome ([[SRC-08]]).

**For this term only.** The line's third sentence says "counts for this term": "as no fetch" and
"as read" say how this term counts a page, not what BEACON keeps of it, so the line puts
no obligation in a definition, and "fetched" and "read" keep their one meaning elsewhere.
[[ACQ-01]], [[ACQ-39]], the Raw page line and the Health entry's count of items BEACON "tried to
fetch and could not" read their own words, unchanged, and do not read this line. F3-04's rule on
what BEACON keeps of such a page is stated by no row, as ruling 3 notes (Out of scope).

**A Drift flag for the unread listing (the owner's answer on the first assessment, 2026-10-06).**
The first assessment found that no row raises a Drift flag for a listing answered with a page that
shows nothing the Entry part locating the listing holds: [[SRC-16]] judges how often an entry part
finds nothing over the items recorded, to which such a Collection adds none, and [[SRC-21]] named
two other events. The owner ruled: "Yes, as a whole-Collection event. Add to SRC-21 in this
proposal: a listing answered with a page showing nothing the Entry part locating the listing holds
raises a Drift flag at that Cycle's close on first occurrence, like an empty listing; after a keep,
judged as a rate over Collections. The Curator then gets the disable / correct / keep decision."
The owner's reasons: "A listing behind a bot wall stops the entire Collection, with no articles and
nothing recorded. That's the same class as an empty listing or every way of fetching refused";
"SRC-04's stopped time and the Health entry record it, but neither asks the Curator to decide";
"the SRC-16 rate test can never see it, because no articles are recorded"; and "After a keep, as
with the other whole-Collection events, it's judged as a rate over Collections, so a source whose
listing is occasionally challenged doesn't nag." Items 4 and 5 carry it. [[SRC-21]] stays one
obligation, a flag for each whole-Collection event a Collection meets, now with a third event under
the conditions the other two have, each of which applies as written: the source enabled when the
Cycle started and when it closes; a Collection that did not begin before a Curator last enabled the
source again; met under the version in force, whose Entry part locating the listing the page is
judged by; no keep of that version for it; no flag on that version for it open. The empty listing's
allowance ("where no listing read under that version before it was put into effect had none") is
not carried: the owner ruled a flag "on first occurrence", and a page that does not show the
listing is never the source's quiet state. The event follows the owner's reasons, "A listing behind
a bot wall stops the entire Collection, with no articles and nothing recorded" and "the SRC-16 rate
test can never see it": it is met only where every page answered for the listing's first page,
retries included, shows nothing the Entry part locating the listing holds. A challenge then read on
a retry, or a later page of a paged listing challenged after earlier pages were read, did not stop
the Collection and is not this event; the latter is a listing not read to its end, which does not
complete. The empty listing is confined to a listing a page answered for it shows the locating part
of, so a challenge page meets one event, not both, and a keep for one leaves the other as it was.
After a keep, [[SRC-16]] judges it as a rate over the source's Collections: the Drift
window line's "for a thing SRC-21 names, the source's Collections" names no event and so needs no
change, nor do the Drift reference and Open (Drift flag) lines. The Drift flag line lists what a
flag can name, so the event joins the two whole-Collection events there.

**Every case has one outcome.** For a Collection that has ended, a listed item BEACON no longer
tries to fetch ([[ACQ-39]]) being left out throughout, and every case but the last two being one
not ended under OPS-15:
- Listing read to its end, at least one listed item BEACON did not hold fetched: completed without
  error, whatever else it failed to fetch, some new items fetched and others not included
  (ruling 1).
- Listing read to its end, some listed item not held, none of those fetched, only held items
  fetched again: not completed without error (ruling 1).
- Listing read to its end, every listed item already held: completed without error, whether or
  not a held item fetched again on a listing signal was fetched.
- Listing read to its end with no entries, its page showing what the Entry part locating the
  listing holds: completed without error (the second branch); [[SRC-21]] decides separately
  whether it raises a Drift flag.
- Listing's first page answered, retries included, only with pages that show nothing the Entry
  part locating the listing holds, a challenge page among them: not read, so not completed without
  error, whatever was fetched ("Not a listing read"); [[SRC-21]] flags the new event, not the empty
  listing, apart from completion (items 4 and 5).
- Listing challenged, then a page answered on a retry shows what the Entry part locating the
  listing holds: read, and judged as the other cases; no flag for the new event.
- A later page of a paged listing answered, retries included, only with such pages, earlier pages
  read: not read to its end, so not completed without error; no flag for the new event.
- After a keep for either listing event, [[SRC-16]] judges that event as a rate over the source's
  Collections, and the other is judged as before; with a flag on the version open for an event, no
  new flag for it.
- Listing read to its end, the only listed items not held being ones BEACON no longer tries to
  fetch: completed without error (the second branch, those items left out; ruling 2).
- Listing read to its end, every listed item not held answered only with a page that shows
  none of what the source's Source Set entry holds as appearing on every page of the source, or
  refused: not completed without error, no such item's content being fetched (ruling 3); where
  every listed item was held, completed without error, as above.
- Listing read to its end, every fetch refused: not completed without error where some listed
  item was not held; completed without error where every listed item was held. [[SRC-21]]'s
  flag for every tried item refused is decided apart from this, in either case.
- Listing not read to its end: not completed without error, whatever was fetched.
- Ended under OPS-15: not completed without error, whatever it read or fetched before.
- A Collection that ends in any way other than under OPS-15 has completed, in [[OPS-15]]'s and
  [[OPS-16]]'s sense, with or without error; so a Collection that ends having fetched nothing
  does not hold its Cycle open until the timeout.
The line keys on how the Collection read, fetched and ended, on what BEACON held and on what it
no longer tries to fetch, none of which it decides.

**The owner's rulings on the draft's choices (2026-10-06).** The draft left seven choices
open, each with options, the text taking the first; the owner ruled each.
1. *A held item fetched again while no new item is.* "No, new must be fetched. Where the listing
   holds an unheld article, only fetching at least one of those counts. A source that can still
   serve old pages but not new ones is failing, which is what SRC-04's time should show. As
   drafted." The owner adds that the amendment's second sentence "states its intent", that "'At
   least one fetch succeeded' was meant for the normal case", and: "A source that fetches some new
   articles but not others still completes. Partial failures are the failure policy's and the
   Health entry's business, not SRC-04's." The line's first branch stands as drafted.
2. *A listed item BEACON no longer tries to fetch ([[ACQ-39]]).* "Leave it out. Articles BEACON
   has stopped trying under ACQ-39 are left out of the test; they are already reported as not
   fetched." Letting one block completion "would freeze SRC-04's time for a working source
   indefinitely, and a quiet source would look dead, the very error SRC-04 exists to prevent",
   and "leaving it out of this test hides nothing": the Health entry counts the items BEACON
   "will not try to fetch again (ACQ-39)". The line's second sentence leaves such an item out of
   both branches.
3. *A page answered with success that shows none of the source's template.* "Not a fetch. Write
   it into the line: such a page is content not fetched (F3-04: it 'never becomes a Raw page, a
   record or a Revision'), so a source serving only challenge pages does not complete." And: "No

   none of what the entry holds as appearing on every page of the source"), naming the entry in

4. *"Completed" alone.* "accept all", with: "Keeping OPS-15 and OPS-16's sense of 'completed'
   for other ended Collections avoids two meanings of one word." The line's last sentence stays.
5. *[[SRC-12]].* "The correction is right. I said this definition 'feeds SRC-12's failure count'
   because the earlier question framed it that way. At HEAD, SRC-12 counts Failed items, not this
   phrase, so SRC-12 stays unchanged."
6. *[[SRC-04]]'s rationale.* "A rationale that explains the definition helps whoever reads
   SRC-04 later. The length is fine." Item 2 replaces it.
7. *The bounds.* "COLLECTION_TIMEOUT's bounds stay unchanged. The edge case needs CYCLE_DEADLINE
   set before 01:00, against a default of 08:00, and the 15-minute default already had its
   equivalent."

**Choices left open.** None. One reading the owner may overrule when ruling: the new event's
scope, set by the owner's stated reasons above, flags only a listing whose first page every answer,
retries included, shows nothing the locating part holds, not a challenge read on retry or a later
page challenged. The one the earlier draft left open, a listing answered with a page
that shows none of what the entry holds, is the owner's answer "Not a listing read" above; whether
it raises a Drift flag is the owner's answer on the first assessment.
