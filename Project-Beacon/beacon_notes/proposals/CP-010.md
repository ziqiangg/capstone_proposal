## Why

**The rulings (2026-10-06).** After [[CP-009]] the owner ruled "three held items first", with
answers on three items held since [[CP-007]] and [[CP-008]]: (1) add one row reporting breaches of
a field set's rules to the Operator view ([[OPS-07]]) and digest ([[OPS-23]]), counted per rule and
source, naming the rule and the field, never the value; (2) restrict [[GOV-17]]'s hand edit,
consistent with [[SRC-08]]'s "only by acceptance": a new version of an existing entry, for anything
a drift test or a Collection reads (the parts and their Standings), enters only by accepting an
Onboarding outcome or a re-checked version BEACON prepared, so that either way it has a Drift
reference, and what nothing judges, such as the source's name, stays hand-editable; (3) count a
possible gap only where the listing was read and listed at least one item, none of them held.
This proposal writes the three down, with the later rulings below; where they stop, it lists its
choices for the assessor.

**The rulings on this draft (2026-10-06).** The owner answered its choices. The breach count: "The
breach count is the Operator's view of BEACON's defects. A breach in a Re-extraction version is
exactly the case the Operator most needs to see", while "The health entry is the Curator's view of
the source"; read as every version, a Re-extraction's included. A rename: "No new version
(Recommended)". The count's granularity and what "read" means for the possible gap: "Accept both
(Recommended)", that is per source, part and field, and the listing read to its end. The gap the
draft found, that no row obliges BEACON to prepare a re-checked version after a Drift flag or gives
the Curator the means to accept one, and that such a version gets no Standings: "Add it to CP-010".
Earlier rulings bound it: "After a drift flag, BEACON prepares the re-checked version with its check
results for the Curator to accept or reject" (F2-02); the Curator "accepts a re-checked version
BEACON prepared" (F2-19); and "It isn't a new source arriving, so it needs no sample, step B or body
share" (CP-007's choices).

**The rulings on the revised choices (2026-10-06).** The owner ruled the six choices the revised
draft listed. A Re-extraction run outside a Cycle: "Option 2, an entry of its own in the next
Operator digest, grouped by run", since "A run outside a Cycle is a release, a correction or a
command. Its breaches are BEACON's defects from that run" and "It's also how failed Re-extraction
runs are already reported (grouped by run and reason)". Which parts are re-read: "Option 3, the
flagged parts, but every part for a whole-Collection event", since "A whole-Collection event
(empty listing, bot wall on the listing, every way of fetching refused) or the address rate (the
site changing its address scheme) usually means the site was redesigned or re-platformed. When
that happens, every part is suspect." A part not re-read: "Keeps its Standing (Recommended)". How
many versions: "One per close (Recommended)". Preparing ended by BEACON's own failure: "Bounded
retry, Operator told (Recommended)". A re-read body, authors or printed-date part the source no
longer offers: "Keep None offered if it had it (Recommended)". So OPS-n1 counts only the versions
a Cycle's record writes recorded and new row OPS-n2 reports a run's; SRC-n2 prepares one version
per close and SRC-n7 re-reads every part but the name for a whole-Collection event or the address rate;
SRC-n3 and the Standing line keep None offered where the flagged version had it; and new rows
SRC-n5 and SRC-n6, modelled on [[SRC-34]] and [[SRC-35]], start a preparing that BEACON's own
failure ends again a bounded number of times and report it once to the Operator when every repeat
fails, the flags staying open meanwhile.

**The rulings on the first assessment (2026-10-06).** The first assessment found nine gaps, six of
them wording, and the owner ruled the other three. A re-checked version with a required part not
established: "Shown, not acceptable (Recommended)". A re-checked version identical to the flagged
one: "Not prepared; entry says so (Recommended)". After the Operator is told preparing kept failing,
in the owner's text: "at each later close while the flag is open, BEACON tries again to prepare a
version, with the same bounded retries. But the Operator is told once, when preparation first fails
every repeat, and not again for that flag until a preparation succeeds or the flag closes. Once the
fault is fixed, a version appears without anyone asking." So SRC-n4 shows a prepared version whose
listing, item-address or fetching part is not established but gives no means to accept it, its flags
staying open; SRC-n2 prepares no version where no Entry part or Standing would change, and
[[SRC-17]]'s entry for each flag it answers says so; SRC-n2 starts a preparing again at each later
close for open flags every preparing for which BEACON's own failure ended, SRC-n5 repeats it with
the same bound at each close, and SRC-n6 reports it once for each flag. The wording gaps are closed
in place: OPS-n1's count is bounded by its own close and, for the first Cycle, takes every record
write completed by its close; OPS-n2 spells out its run as [[ACQ-37]] and [[GOV-06]] do; SRC-n2
reads the pages under the whole prepared version and new row SRC-n7 states its re-reading in the
rows' own terms, naming the Drift sign it means; the Standing line and SRC-n3 agree on None offered
carried through prepared versions; the A1 Curator line and [[GOV-17]]'s rationale name Standings;
and the case table is corrected for [[SRC-21]] and [[SRC-33]].

**1. Breaches to the Operator.** At HEAD a field whose found or supplied value breaks a rule is
held with no kept value, its reason naming the part it fails ([[ACQ-12]]; A-terms, Reason for no
kept value), and the Curator's Health entry counts such fields by reason ([[SRC-19]]). No row tells
the Operator, though the owner's earlier ruling sends breaches there as defects. The vault's term
