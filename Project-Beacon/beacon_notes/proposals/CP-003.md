# ACQ-n03

WHEN BEACON removes anything under ACQ-n01 or ACQ-n02, BEACON shall remove every Copy of it that BEACON holds outside a backup, apart from a value that the item's Article Record still holds, by the end of the first Cycle that ends after the erasure is made, and in any case within 24 hours of it.

## Why

The owner's rulings on the [[Q-019]] forms (2026-10-04), the retention ruling of
2026-10-04, the trim ruling of the same day, and the rulings of 2026-10-05 on the
integrated plan's open items are the evidence. They do not answer [[Q-019]] or
[[Q-026]]; they say how long BEACON keeps what it collects and what an erasure must
reach, and [[ACQ-02]], [[ACQ-07]], [[ACQ-08]], [[A-terms]] and the two parameters as
they stand cannot say it. This is the second proposal of the chain those rulings name,
after [[CP-002]]. Each paragraph below names the item of the integrated plan's group 3
it carries.

**Raw pages kept, and what they are (items 1 and 3; F1-02, F1-03, the retention ruling,
trim item 17).** [[ACQ-02]] keeps a raw page only until [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] has passed.
The owner ruled option (ii) of the retention research: keep every record and its text
indefinitely and keep raw pages instead of erasing them at the horizon ("yes, go with
(ii)"). The trim then ruled no RAW_PAGE_RETENTION parameter: raw pages are kept as a
rule. [[ACQ-02]] now keeps the raw page with no horizon, and its rationale no longer
says the page goes when re-derivation stops reaching it. The owner ruled that the Raw
page is everything a Collection returned for the item that is read to extract its text or
decide its fields, the rendered copy and the listing entry included (F1-03 (b)), and that
these share the raw page's retention and erasure; the A2 entry now says so. The owner
ruled that a Revision's raw page is kept as well as a new item's (F1-02 (b)), which
[[ACQ-02]] does not cover, since it speaks of items BEACON does not already hold;
[[ACQ-06]] now records a Revision with its raw page. The owner ruled that the
obligation to keep raw pages lives "In the rows", so the A2 entry stays a definition.

**[[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] bounds re-derivation only (item 2).** With raw pages kept, the
parameter no longer says how long a page is kept; its bounds now state only how far back
re-derivation reaches. Its default and its body are unchanged, and [[FBK-08]],
[[FBK-09]], [[FBK-10]] and [[FBK-15]] still link it.

**[[ACQ-07]]'s rationale (item 3).** It said the text of an excluded item has no use
beyond the re-derivation horizon. Under the retention ruling it is kept by default
(item 4), and erased only where a Curator chooses a duration. The rationale now says so;
the row is unchanged.

**[[EXCLUDED_TEXT_RETENTION]] defaults to never (item 4; the retention ruling).** Its
bounds already allow `never`, so only the default changes, and the body no longer says
the default names [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]].

**What an erasure reaches (item 6; F5-03 (a), the trim ruling, and the ruling of
2026-10-05 "Say it, as needs").** The owner ruled that an erasure removes the text of
every version that exists at the time and any pending text, and that under [[ACQ-08]] it
also removes every raw page, the rendered copy and the listing entry included; the field
sets stay whole, with their strings as found. The ruling on the delivered values (the
integrated plan's open item 21, "Keep, with Q-018 note") added that an [[ACQ-08]] erasure
reaches the values kept apart under [[ACQ-12]]. ACQ-n01 carries the text, for an erasure
under either row; ACQ-n02 carries the raw pages and the values kept apart, under
[[ACQ-08]] only, keeping the rest of the record. The owner then ruled "Say it, as needs":
an erasure reaches every copy of the text BEACON holds, backups included, by a stated
expiry and by replaying erasures after a restore; chunks and vectors are copies and are
covered without naming their design. The A2 entry Copy says what a copy is by what it
holds; ACQ-n03 removes every Copy outside a backup; ACQ-n04 gives each backup a stated
expiry; ACQ-n05 makes erasures again after a restore. None names how a backup is made or
how a Stage removes a Chunk: the trim ruled that backups are designed outside [[Q-026]].
On the draft's open choices the owner ruled on 2026-10-05: a quotation in a Judgement or
Account is "Not a Copy. Copy means what is made to store, search or retrieve the text
(chunks, vectors, caches, exports). A quotation in BEACON's own Judgement or Account is
evidence, governed by its own rows (correction, withdrawal); an erasure flags it for the
Curator but does not silently rewrite published output", citing [[EVD-03]] and
[[UIX-11]]. The Copy entry now says so, and ACQ-n10 flags each such Judgement and
Account in the Curator digest. Copies outside a backup go "Within the Cycle", as ACQ-n03
says; after the first assessment the owner bounded it: "Cycle, at most 24 hours. By the
end of the first Cycle that ends after the erasure, and in any case within 24 hours of
it. One fixed number, matching the daily Cycle", since "A takedown can't wait
indefinitely just because Cycles are paused". The Copy entry also reaches the values
kept apart under [[ACQ-12]], which ACQ-n02 removes, and [[ACQ-12]]'s rationale now says
an erasure removes them, as [[ACQ-02]]'s says of the raw page; a backup's expiry is "Stated per backup", as ACQ-n04 says; and a restore makes
again both erasures and lifts, in order ("Replay both"), as ACQ-n05 says.

**An erasure persists, and a Curator may lift it (item 5; F5-03 (a), the ruling "Only
ACQ-08 persists", and the ruling of 2026-10-05 on lifting).** [[ACQ-08]] as it stands
erases the text and the page once; the next Collection that returns the item would keep
them again. The owner ruled that an erased item that a later Collection returns is
recorded as an Encounter only, its content not kept again and no Revision recorded,

**Notes for the assessor.**
- *Placeholders.* The grammar asks only for distinct placeholders, and `apply` replaces

  order.

- *A digest is not a Copy (item 8, a note).* The record's plan assumes an erased item is

  recognised. A digest reproduces no part of the text and is not made to store, search

- [[ACQ-10]]'s rationale still says a reviewer can judge a field "after the raw page has

- An item whose text [[ACQ-07]] erased keeps its raw page, so its return can still be

- ACQ-n01 reaches only versions that exist at the erasure, as F5-03 rules ("every version
