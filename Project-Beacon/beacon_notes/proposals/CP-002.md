# CP-002 ACQ-02 has no unknown, no credit to the publisher, and no line on what a source states

## The change

[[ACQ-02]] is split into eight rows, each with one obligation, and the field states and the reasons are moved into [[A-terms]] A2 as terms. Seven replacements: [[ACQ-02]]'s requirement sentence and its rationale (the first part of the split, keeping the id), three lines of [[A-terms]] A2 (the Supplied value line becoming three lines, adding two terms, and the Author line's citation), and [[GOV-18]]'s requirement sentence and its rationale. Seven new rows in group ACQ, under the placeholders ACQ-n1 to ACQ-n7, each taking the next free number unless the owner assigns one; `apply` replaces each placeholder everywhere it appears in this change. No parameter changes. The frontmatter and **Verification** line of [[ACQ-02]] and [[GOV-18]] are unchanged.

**1. [[ACQ-02]], the requirement, as it stands:**

```
BEACON shall retain an Article Record for each Discovered item it does not already hold, containing its extracted text; its title, Item publisher, source, URL and collection time; every Author and its Source publication time wherever the source gives them; for each of its title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time, where each value was found, and every other value the source gives for it where those values disagree, the value BEACON keeps being the item's, or that the source gives none; and its raw page until [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] has passed since its Collection. Where the source gives no title, BEACON shall supply one. BEACON shall record in the item's Article Record a title, author or publication time that it supplies where the source gives none as a Supplied value, never as given by the source, with the procedure that supplied it and that procedure's version, which changes whenever the procedure, its model, the wording of its prompt or its settings change, and, where a model supplied it, the model's identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
BEACON shall retain an Article Record for each Discovered item it does not already hold, containing its extracted text; its source, URL and collection time; its title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time, each in a Field state (ACQ-n1); where the item credits as its author its Item publisher, or its source where the source is recorded in place of its Item publisher, that it does, that credit never being recorded among its Authors; and its raw page until [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] has passed since its Collection.
```

**2. [[ACQ-02]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** Every later Stage reads these fields (ACQ-03). Keeping them for items that are not admitted lets a scope decision be re-derived after the Scope Criteria change. A source often omits its Authors and its publication time, so they are kept as the source's only where it gives them; an item may credit several Authors, and each is kept. The Item publisher is kept apart from the Authors, so that a site's own name is never read as a credit. A later row relies on every item having a title (ACQ-07), so BEACON supplies one where the source gives none. Where each value was found, and every value where the source contradicts itself, let a reviewer judge how far to trust a field without fetching the page again, and after the raw page has gone. A value BEACON supplies is recorded as supplied, with the procedure and version that supplied it and, for a model, its identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings, so that it is never read as what the source said and can be traced to what produced it. ACQ-06 keeps each Revision's prior version. The raw page is kept for as long as re-derivation can reach back, so that a release can extract the text again from it (ACQ-09), for instance to follow the item's headings, lists and tables more closely; after that, the extracted text is the evidence of record.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
**Rationale.** Every later Stage reads these fields (ACQ-03). Keeping them for items that are not admitted lets a scope decision be re-derived after the Scope Criteria change. The Item publisher is kept apart from the Authors, so that a site's own name is never read as a credit; where an item credits its Item publisher, or the source recorded in place of it, the record says so and the credit is read as the publisher's own, never as an Author. ACQ-06 keeps each Revision's prior version. The raw page is kept for as long as re-derivation can reach back, so that a release can extract the text again from it (ACQ-09), for instance to follow the item's headings, lists and tables more closely; after that, the extracted text is the evidence of record.
```

**3. [[A-terms]] A2, the Source publication time line, as it stands:**

```
| Source publication time | The time a source states as the time it published. A time BEACON reads from what a page shows or works out by any other means, where the source states none, is not a Source publication time but a Supplied value (ACQ-02). |
```

**Replaced by:**

```
| Source publication time | The time a source states as the time it published. A source states it where the item's page declares it in its metadata, or where the source's own listing of its items gives a date for an item it publishes itself, in whatever form; a date the source gives as when the item last changed is never a publication time. A time the source states less precisely than a day, such as a month, a quarter or a year, is a Source publication time at that precision. A date BEACON reads from what the item's page prints or from its URL, or a time it works out by any other means, where the source states none, is not a Source publication time but a Supplied value (ACQ-n4). Where the only time the source states cannot be read without guessing, the item has no Source publication time and BEACON supplies none: its Article Record records the time as unknown (ACQ-n1). Where the source also states a time that can be read, that time is kept, and the one that cannot is recorded only as found, beside it (ACQ-n1). |
```

**4. [[A-terms]] A2, the Supplied value line, as it stands:**

```
| Supplied value | A title, author or publication time that BEACON records for an item where its source gives none (ACQ-02). |
```

**Replaced by** (three lines, in this order):

```
| Supplied value | A title, author or publication time that BEACON records for an item where its source states none that BEACON can keep (ACQ-n4). |
| Field state | How an Article Record holds each of an item's title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time (ACQ-n1): one of three. *Kept*: the value BEACON keeps, which the source states and is the item's (for the Authors, every Author the source states that BEACON can read without guessing), with every other value the source states for it where those values disagree; a value the source states that cannot be read without guessing is held only as found, with that reason, and is never the kept value. *None stated*: that the source states none, with, for the title, a title BEACON supplies; for the Item publisher, the item's source as its Item publisher; and, for the Authors or the Source publication time, a value BEACON supplies, where it supplies one. *No kept value*: where BEACON can keep no value the source states for it for a Reason for no kept value, that reason and every value the source states for it that BEACON found, with, for the Authors or the Source publication time, that its value is unknown; for the title, a title BEACON supplies; and, for the Item publisher, the item's source recorded in place of its Item publisher, never as its Item publisher. A title and an Item publisher are never unknown. |
| Reason for no kept value | One of a closed list of circumstances concerning a field of an Article Record (ACQ-n1, ACQ-n2, ACQ-n3): a place where the value would be found could not be read, the place being named; a value the source states cannot be read without guessing; a part of the item was not obtained, the part being named; the value cannot be derived from what BEACON retains of the item; or what BEACON found or supplied for it does not meet ACQ-02, ACQ-n1, ACQ-n2, ACQ-n3, ACQ-n4, ACQ-n5, ACQ-n6 or ACQ-n7 or the terms they use, the part it fails being named. |
```

**6. [[GOV-18]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** A Method's model, prompt and settings decide every Judgement it produces; they change only with a new release of BEACON, which is tested and versioned as a whole, and GOV-07 attributes the change to that release.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
**Rationale.** A Method's model, prompt and settings decide every Judgement it produces, and a procedure that supplies values under ACQ-n1 or ACQ-n4 decides fields that every later Stage reads (ACQ-03). Each changes only with a new release of BEACON, which is tested and versioned as a whole; GOV-07 attributes a Method's change to that release, and a supplying procedure's change shows in the version that each value it supplies records (ACQ-n4, ACQ-n5). What a Curator changes for a source between releases is its Source Set entry, which is not part of such a procedure's settings (ACQ-n5).
```

## Why

**This form: single-obligation rows (2026-10-05).** The third form was assessed
coherent. The owner then agreed with its content but found [[ACQ-02]] too long for a
functional requirement, and ruled "Restructure": split [[ACQ-02]] into short rows of one
obligation each and move the field states and the reasons into [[A-terms]] as terms.
The content is unchanged from the coherent third form; only its form changes. The first
part of the split keeps [[ACQ-02]]'s id and holds the list of contents; ACQ-n1 holds the four fields in a Field state; ACQ-n2 bars
"the source states none" where a reason applies; ACQ-n3 keeps the values found or
supplied on a breach; ACQ-n4 records a Supplied value; ACQ-n5 records a procedure by its
version; ACQ-n6 records the text's reasons; ACQ-n7 bounds what the text is without a
readable text layer. The A2 terms Field state and Reason for no
kept value carry the three states and the closed list of reasons.

The restructured form was assessed coherent with five gaps, all of wording, and this
form meets them: the bar and the kept values are two rows, not one; the text's reasons
are one "WHERE ... BEACON shall" row in the group's form; the breach that keeps the
values names every row of the split and the terms they use, as the Reason for no kept
value term does; the kept state holds every Author the source states; and the A2 entries
and [[GOV-18]] cite the split row that now holds what they cite. The restructured form
had moved the description kept once per version, and the Source Set entry not being a
procedure's settings, into a decision; the owner ruled on 2026-10-05 that they come
"Back into CP-002", as ACQ-n5, the one-line clarification F1-19 asked for, with the
version of the Source Set entry recorded beside the procedure's version (F5-07).
How the description is produced (generated at build and committed with the release) is
the design's and goes in a decision to follow, after this proposal applies.
The Field state, bound by ACQ-n1, obliges every Author the source states that BEACON can
read without guessing; an Author it cannot read is recorded only as found, beside the
kept ones, and is never kept (F1-13).

The single-obligation form was assessed coherent with four gaps, all of wording that
follows rulings already made, and this form meets them. ACQ-n7 restores, as its own row,
the bound the earlier forms carried: without a readable text layer the extracted text is
only what is extracted from another form the source offers, or nothing (F3-08, "Check
plus converter test"). The Reason for no kept value term is now a closed list of
circumstances, not a reason for a state, and ACQ-n2 is keyed on a circumstance holding
where BEACON keeps no stated value, so it no longer presupposes the state it governs.
[[ACQ-02]] lists the four fields, each in a Field state, without restating what the
kept state holds, so every readable Author and a stated Source publication time are
obliged once, through ACQ-n1; the sentence on Authors in the rationale moves with them
to ACQ-n1. The A2 Author entry is now named, its "Supplied value" citation pointed at
ACQ-n4, and nothing else in it changes.

The owner's rulings on the [[Q-019]] forms (2026-10-04) and on the open items of the
integrated plan (2026-10-05) are the evidence. They do not answer [[Q-019]] or
[[Q-026]]; they say what an Article Record must be able to say, and [[ACQ-02]],
[[A-terms]] and [[GOV-18]] as applied by [[CP-001]] cannot say it. This proposal is the
first of the chain those rulings name; it carries only the metadata and provenance
changes. Each paragraph below names the form or item it carries.

**What a source states (form answers F1-07, F1-08, F1-12, F1-13, F4-02).** A2 says a
Source publication time is what a source "states", but not which dates it states. The
owner ruled (F1-07, option 2) that declared metadata and any date the source's own
listing gives for the item, whatever its form, are stated, "for entries the source
itself publishes", and that a date printed on the item's page or read from its URL is
Supplied; that a month the source states is a Source publication time at month
precision (F1-08), widened by the owner on 2026-10-05 to any span the source states
("Any span, at its precision": a quarter or a year counts, at that precision); that "gives" in [[ACQ-02]] is read as "states", as A-terms reads the
publication time, for the four fields and the Supplied clause (F1-12); and that a time
the source states that cannot be put into one form without guessing is "unknown" with
that reason, not supplied, "since the source did state a time" (F1-13, F4-02). The A2
entries and every "gives" in [[ACQ-02]]'s field and Supplied clauses now say so. The
entry also says a date given as the item's last change never counts: the owner ruled
that a sitemap's modification date is never recorded as a publication time, stated or
supplied (F1-10).

**No "unknown" (F1-15, F5-09; the integrated plan's open item 20).** [[ACQ-02]] lets a
field hold a value or "that the source gives none", nothing else. Where BEACON could not
read a place, could not read a value without guessing, did not obtain a part of the
item, or cannot derive a value from what it retains, the row as it stands forces it to
say the source gives none, which is false. The owner ruled one "unknown" marker, with
one reason from a stated list, which "never says the source gives none in those cases"
(F1-15). The owner ruled that a breach of the row's shape, other than the title,
writes that field as "unknown" with the breached rule as its reason, and that its
values as found are kept, "so the record never states anything false" (F5-09). The row
now holds, per field, exactly one of a kept value, "the source states none" and
"unknown" with its reason; other values may sit beside a kept value, as before, or
beside an unknown one, because a value that cannot be read without guessing is kept
only as found, among the other values, and is never the kept value (F1-13; the
integrated plan's finding on F5-09, ruled with the plan). The reasons are listed in
words, closed: place not read, not readable without guessing, part not obtained, not
derivable from what is retained, and a breach of this row. A title is never unknown
(F5-09: "a title cannot be unknown").

**The text's reasons (F3-08; the integrated plan's open item 3).** The owner ruled that a document
with no text layer has empty text, or what a further fetch step gives (for arXiv, the
abstract), with the reason "no text layer" recorded and the PDF kept (F3-08), and that a
text layer the converter garbles is rejected as an "unreadable text layer" (ruled
2026-10-05 as "Check plus converter test"). The owner ruled on 2026-10-05 ("Also the
field reasons") that the extracted text may also be recorded as empty with "a part was
not obtained" or "cannot be derived from what is retained" where those apply, beside its
own two. The row now says each of these reasons is recorded with the extracted text.

**No credit to the publisher (F1-16).** The owner ruled that where an item credits its
Item publisher as author, the record says that it did; the name is not an Author. The
row as it stands has nowhere to say it. The row now keeps that fact.

**An Item publisher under a breach (the integrated plan's open item 19).** A breach written
"unknown" would leave an item with no Item publisher, which A-terms does not allow. The
owner ruled (2026-10-05, "Source stands"): the item's source stands, as A-terms already
says where an item names none, and the breach is recorded; no term changes. The rework
below widens this to every reason and words it as recording the source in place of the
Item publisher.

**This form, after assessment (the owner's rulings of 2026-10-05).** The first form was
assessed coherent with three gaps; this form meets them.
- *A time that cannot be read, beside one that can.* The Source publication time entry
  said that a time that cannot be read without guessing leaves the item with none, with
  no exception for a second stated time that can be read. F1-13 rules "unknown" only
  where such a value "is the only value the source states". The entry now says so, and
  that otherwise the readable time is kept and the other recorded only as found beside
  it, as [[ACQ-02]] says. No ruling was needed.
- *A title or an Item publisher under a reason other than a breach.* Neither may be
  unknown, yet the first form gave neither a permitted state where a place could not be
  read, a value could not be read without guessing, a part was not obtained or a value
  could not be derived. For the title the owner ruled "Supply it": where no stated title
  can be kept, BEACON supplies one, recorded as a Supplied value with the reason. The
  row and the A2 Supplied value entry now say "where the source states none that BEACON
  can keep"; neither names a procedure. For the Item publisher the owner ruled "Source
  stands for all": for any of the reasons, a breach included, the record holds the
  item's source with the reason, never "unknown".
- *The source recorded as the Item publisher against A2.* Where the item names another
  organisation, A2 makes that organisation the Item publisher, so the first form's
  "record the item's source as its Item publisher" conflicted with the term. The row and
  its rationale now record the source in place of the Item publisher, with the reason,
  so no term changes (the same ruling, "Source stands for all").
- *The assessor's observation on "then".* "Shall then never record that the source
  states none" now reads "where one of these reasons applies, it shall never record that
  the source states none".

**The delivered values (the integrated plan's open item 21).** "Its values as found are kept"
(F5-09) does not say where; a breached field may hold a Supplied value, which cannot sit
among the source's values. The owner ruled (2026-10-05, "Keep, with Q-018 note") that
the field set as delivered is kept beside the written one, and that this proposal says
so. The row now keeps every value found or supplied, as it stood, apart from the fields
it records.

**A description recorded once (F1-19, F5-07; the integrated plan's open item 58).** The owner
ruled that a Supplied value carries its procedure's version, and the description is
held once per version in the release's procedures file, "kept as long as any record
cites it and exported with the records that cite it"; that reading a cited description
as recorded in the item's Article Record is contestable, so a one-line clarification is
listed (F1-19, option b); and that a source's configuration is not part of a
procedure's settings, that reading joining the same clarification (F5-07). The owner
ruled on 2026-10-05 that the model clause is kept as applied ("Keep it"), and that the
procedures file is generated at build from each procedure's description, which is the
design's and is not written into the row. ACQ-n5 now says what is recorded where, and
that a Source Set entry is not a procedure's settings; ACQ-n4's model clause is unchanged.

**A supplying procedure no role may change (F5-08).** [[GOV-18]] keeps a Method out of
every role's reach, and a supplying procedure is not a Method. The owner ruled that a
supplying procedure "changes only with a release, as a Method does", since it decides
fields every later Stage reads, and that one line extends [[GOV-18]] to it. The
rationale keeps [[GOV-07]]'s attribution to a Method only, since [[GOV-07]] covers
Governed Artefacts and a supplying procedure is not one; such a procedure's change shows
in the version its values record.

**This form, after the second assessment (2026-10-05).** The second form was assessed
coherent with five gaps; this form meets them.
- *Which state each case holds.* The row said "exactly one of" three states that did not
  cover a supplied title or the source recorded in place of the Item publisher. It now
  names three states and what each holds per field: the kept value; that the source
  states none, with a supplied title always, the item's source as Item publisher as A2
  provides, and a supplied author or time where one is supplied; and, where no stated
  value can be kept for a reason, the reason, with "unknown" for the Authors and the
  Source publication time, a supplied title, or the source recorded in place of the
  Item publisher.
- *A reason fires only where nothing can be kept.* The third state applies only where
  BEACON can keep no value the source states; where another can be kept, it is kept (the
  ordered places of F4-03 and the publisher chain of F4-05), and an unreadable value is
  recorded beside it as found.
- *The values as found under a title breach.* The clause keeping every value found or
  supplied now fires on the third state from a breach, which includes a title supplied
  because the one found breaks the row (the open item 21 ruling, "Keep, with Q-018 note",
  with "Supply it").
- *No export.* "And exports it with every record that names it" is removed: F1-19 asked
  for no export, and no row gives one.
- *A credit to the source in place.* The owner ruled on 2026-10-05, "Credit to the
  publisher": where the source is recorded in place of the Item publisher, an item's
  credit to it is recorded as a credit to the publisher (F1-16) and is not an Author.
  The row says so, and that the credit is never recorded among the Authors, so no term
