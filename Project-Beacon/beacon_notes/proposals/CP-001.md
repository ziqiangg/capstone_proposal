# CP-001 ACQ-02 keeps one author, no publisher and no provenance

## The change

Four replacements: [[ACQ-02]]'s requirement sentence, [[ACQ-02]]'s rationale, and two
lines of [[A-terms]] A2. No row is added and no parameter changes. [[ACQ-02]]'s
frontmatter and its **Verification** line are unchanged.

**1. [[ACQ-02]], the requirement, as it stands:**

```
BEACON shall retain an Article Record for each Discovered item it does not already hold, containing its extracted text, its title, source, URL and collection time, its author and Source publication time wherever the source gives them, and its raw page until [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] has passed since its Collection.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
BEACON shall retain an Article Record for each Discovered item it does not already hold, containing its extracted text; its title, Item publisher, source, URL and collection time; every Author and its Source publication time wherever the source gives them; for each of its title, Item publisher, Authors and Source publication time, where each value was found, and every other value the source gives for it where those values disagree, the value BEACON keeps being the item's, or that the source gives none; and its raw page until [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] has passed since its Collection. Where the source gives no title, BEACON shall supply one. BEACON shall record in the item's Article Record a title, author or publication time that it supplies where the source gives none as a Supplied value, never as given by the source, with the procedure that supplied it and that procedure's version, which changes whenever the procedure, its model, the wording of its prompt or its settings change, and, where a model supplied it, the model's identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings.
```

**2. [[ACQ-02]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** Every later Stage reads these fields (ACQ-03). Keeping them for items that are not admitted lets a scope decision be re-derived after the Scope Criteria change. A source often omits the author and the publication time, so both are kept only where given. ACQ-06 keeps each Revision's prior version. The raw page is kept for as long as re-derivation can reach back, so that a release can extract the text again from it (ACQ-09), for instance to follow the item's headings, lists and tables more closely; after that, the extracted text is the evidence of record.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
**Rationale.** Every later Stage reads these fields (ACQ-03). Keeping them for items that are not admitted lets a scope decision be re-derived after the Scope Criteria change. A source often omits its Authors and its publication time, so they are kept as the source's only where it gives them; an item may credit several Authors, and each is kept. The Item publisher is kept apart from the Authors, so that a site's own name is never read as a credit. A later row relies on every item having a title (ACQ-07), so BEACON supplies one where the source gives none. Where each value was found, and every value where the source contradicts itself, let a reviewer judge how far to trust a field without fetching the page again, and after the raw page has gone. A value BEACON supplies is recorded as supplied, with the procedure and version that supplied it and, for a model, its identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings, so that it is never read as what the source said and can be traced to what produced it. ACQ-06 keeps each Revision's prior version. The raw page is kept for as long as re-derivation can reach back, so that a release can extract the text again from it (ACQ-09), for instance to follow the item's headings, lists and tables more closely; after that, the extracted text is the evidence of record.
```

**3. [[A-terms]] A2, the Source publication time line, as it stands:**

```
| Source publication time | The time a source says it published. |
```

**Replaced by** (four lines, in this order):

```
| Author | A person, or an organisation other than the item's Item publisher, that an item credits as its author or as having jointly released it. An item may have several Authors, or none. A name BEACON records where the item credits none is a Supplied value (ACQ-02), not an Author. |
| Item publisher | What publishes an item: the organisation the item names as publishing it or, where it names none, the item's source as its Article Record keeps it (ACQ-02). It is never an Author of that item, even where the item credits it. |
| Source publication time | The time a source states as the time it published. A time BEACON reads from what a page shows or works out by any other means, where the source states none, is not a Source publication time but a Supplied value (ACQ-02). |
| Supplied value | A title, author or publication time that BEACON records for an item where its source gives none (ACQ-02). |
```

**4. [[A-terms]] A2, the Judgement line, as it stands:**

```
| Judgement | Any determination BEACON makes rather than copies from a source. It is one of a closed list: scope decision, item\_type, subject tags, development membership, claim alignment and agreement, assessment, mapping, trend, selection, statement class, claim extraction, entity extraction. |
```

**Replaced by:**

```
| Judgement | Any determination BEACON makes rather than copies from a source. It is one of a closed list: scope decision, item\_type, subject tags, development membership, claim alignment and agreement, assessment, mapping, trend, selection, statement class, claim extraction, entity extraction. Recording an Article Record's fields (ACQ-02) is not a Judgement, including where BEACON keeps one of several values a source gives or records a Supplied value. |
```

## Why

[[X-006]] (recorded; it supersedes [[X-005]]) is the evidence, with the owner's rulings
of 2026-10-01 on the author route ("A and B together") and the label rule ("Site =
publisher; joint lists are authors"). It does not answer [[Q-019]]; it shows what the
record has to be able to hold, and [[ACQ-02]] as it stands cannot hold it.

This is the proposal's seventh form. Assessments of the first two, read by the owner and
not recorded, each found it coherent with gaps: seven in the first form, three in the
second; the third was found coherent, and the owner then ruled that a Supplied value
must be traceable to what supplied it; assessments of the fourth and fifth forms each found
three gaps in how that was worded. The sixth was found coherent, and the owner then
asked that settings be added and three wordings be made clearer. The owner ruled on each open choice (2026-10-01), and this form carries those
rulings. "How the earlier forms' gaps are met" below takes them in turn.

**One author.** The row keeps "its author". Items credit several: arXiv papers list
their authors, and a joint advisory lists 26 releasing organisations. The row now keeps
every Author.

**No publisher.** The owner ruled that the site's own organisation is the publisher,
never an author. In [[X-006]], five of the six items gained over [[X-005]]'s design
on like-for-like labels are that rule. [[ACQ-02]] has no field for it, and
[[A-terms]] defines neither word, so the rule binds nothing. The row adds the Item
publisher, and two A2 entries define Author and Item publisher, the second saying it is
never an Author. The Author entry keeps organisations other than the site, and jointly
releasing agencies, as Authors, per the label rule. The term is "Item publisher", by
the owner's ruling, because [[CTX-01]], [[CTX-09]], [[CTX-12]] and [[D-003]] already
use "publisher" for the organisation that releases a corpus; those rows are untouched.
Where an item names no publisher, the item's source, which its Article Record already
keeps, stands as its Item publisher, by the owner's ruling. So every item has an Item
publisher, none is a Supplied value, and no Source Set row has to hold a name.

**No provenance.** The row says what to keep, not where it came from or whether the
source agrees with itself. [[X-006]]'s known limits show why that matters: most dates
rest on one source (49 of 58 kept items), a declared name the page never shows is
still reported, and agreement is not truth (check S123). A reviewer who cannot see
where a value came from cannot judge it, which is problem **D**. The row now keeps,
per field, where each value was found; every other value the source gives where its
values disagree, by the owner's ruling, since the raw page lapses at
[[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] and a mark alone could not be reviewed afterwards; or that the
source gives none. The value BEACON keeps is the item's value, so [[UIX-05]] and
[[UIX-06]] still read one Source publication time per item. The row names no tier, no
voter, no selector and none of the design's status words: those are the design's, and
[[Q-019]]'s answer may change them.

**A title is always kept.** The owner ruled that the title stays mandatory: where the
source gives none, BEACON supplies one, recorded as a Supplied value. [[ACQ-07]]'s
rationale relies on a title being there.

**Source publication time.** A2 defines it as "the time a source says it published".
[[X-006]]'s design takes a date from a heuristic where neither the page's declared
metadata nor the listing gives one (7 of 58 kept items), and may fill an empty title or
date from a model call. The owner ruled that a date the page shows but does not state
as its publication time is BEACON's inference, not the source's statement. The entry
is kept strict and says so: such a time is a Supplied value, not a Source publication
time. So [[UIX-05]] and [[UIX-06]] filter and sort only on stated times, and
[[ACQ-01]]'s rationale already distrusts even those. Whether Readers should also
filter on supplied times is a change to those rows, not made here.

**Not a Judgement.** A2 says a Judgement is "any determination BEACON makes rather than
copies from a source", and closes the list without one for metadata. A heuristic date,
a model-filled title and the choice among a source's disagreeing values are each
something BEACON works out, so as it stands the entry is silent on whether they are
Judgements. The owner ruled that a value BEACON supplies is not one, and, separately,
that keeping one of a source's disagreeing values is not one. The Judgement entry now says so in
terms: recording an Article Record's fields is not a Judgement, including keeping one
of several values and recording a Supplied value. What this gives up is stated
plainly: a Supplied value has no decision record ([[EVD-01]]), no Method version
([[GOV-18]]) and falls under no trigger ([[GOV-06]], [[D-002]]); a Curator's power to
correct a Judgement does not reach it. What it keeps is [[ACQ-02]]'s own obligation:
the value is recorded as supplied, with the procedure that supplied it and its
version and, for a model, its identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings. The
owner ruled that strengthening on 2026-10-01, having weighed the cost, and then that
settings be added and the prompt be named by its wording, so that the record holds the
procedure's standing prompt and no item text ([[ACQ-07]], [[ACQ-08]]): it lets a supplied value be traced to
what produced it, without the correction and re-derivation rows. The row itself says
when that version changes, since no row versions a procedure other than a Method and
such a procedure is not one. It also says the Supplied value is recorded in the item's
Article Record, since by the new entries a supplied name is not an Author and a
supplied time is not a Source publication time. Who may
correct a metadata value, copied or supplied, and whether Readers see that one was
supplied, is [[Q-025]]. [[Q-024]] holds whether a model call may ever decide a field.

**No limit on which fields may be supplied.** The owner ruled for [[X-006]]'s design
that a model may fill a title or a date, never authors. By the owner's ruling the row
does not repeat that: it obliges a supplied title, permits a supplied author or
publication time, and leaves which fields a design fills, and by what means, to the
design's own ruling.

### How the earlier forms' gaps are met

- **[[FBK-23]]** (a corrected determination reaching Readers unmarked), **[[EVD-03]]**
  and the Evidence locator entry (a value with no passage to locate), **[[FBK-20]]**,
  and **[[FBK-17]], [[FBK-13]], [[FBK-18]], [[UIX-10]]** through the Revised result
  entry: each arose only because a supplied value was a Judgement. It is not one, so
  none of these rows is touched.
- **"publisher" used for a corpus:** the new term is "Item publisher".
- **"its title" unconditional while the row provided for none:** the title is
  mandatory and the row says BEACON supplies it.
- **Which disagreeing value is the item's:** the row says the value BEACON keeps.
- **Choosing among disagreeing values is a determination:** the Judgement entry says it
  is not a Judgement, by the owner's ruling on that choice.
- **(Second form) No row gives a Source Set entry a name:** the Item publisher falls
  back to the source the Article Record keeps, not to an entry's name.
- **(Second form) A supplied Author is not one the item credits:** the Author entry
  says such a name is a Supplied value, and the row and the Supplied value entry write
  "author" in lower case.

**Notes for the assessor.**
- The row obliges a title for every item and says nothing of an item for which BEACON
  can supply none. [[X-006]]'s only means of supplying a title is a model call that no
  run has measured ([[Q-024]]); on its 58 kept test pages no title was empty.
- Under the strict entry, an item whose time is supplied has no Source publication
  time, so [[UIX-05]] and [[UIX-06]] neither filter nor sort it by time.
- [[Q-019]]'s "What is unknown" says keeping every author and labelling provenance are
  design choices it adds. If this is applied, that sentence is stale; restating the
  question is a later `ask`, not part of this change.

### Out of scope

- **[[SRC-03]], [[SRC-12]], [[SRC-14]] and selectors.** Whether "the details a Collection
  needs" includes extraction details such as a byline selector, and whether a selector
  that silently stops matching should be counted or flagged, belong to the
  source-configuration step that [[X-006]] assumes and nobody has designed; [[Q-020]]
  asks it.
- **[[UIX-05]] and [[UIX-06]]**: filtering or sorting by Author or Item publisher is not
  added.
- **[[Q-018]]**: keeping every Author keeps more names of persons; the privacy question
  stays where it is.
- **[[Q-019]]**: this proposal does not answer it, and names no extraction design.

## Assessment

Assessed on the seventh form as it stands in the working tree, against the rows as they
stand. Read: [[ACQ-02]], [[A-terms]], ACQ-01 and ACQ-03 to ACQ-09, EVD-01 to EVD-03,
GOV-05 to GOV-07, GOV-18, FBK-01, FBK-17, FBK-20, FBK-23, UIX-05 to UIX-07, the SRC and
CTX rows that name a source or a publisher, [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]], D-001 to D-003,
[[X-006]], [[Q-019]], [[Q-025]], and the owner's rulings of 2026-10-01. No decision
cites [[ACQ-02]]. `beacon_notes/proposals/` holds no other proposal, so there is no
sibling. The earlier assessments the Why recounts are not recorded anywhere read here
and were not checked, except that the rulings confirm the sixth form was assessed
coherent.

**[[ACQ-02]], the requirement.**
1. *Coherent.* The "as it stands" quote is exact. The new text does what the Why
   claims: every Author; an Item publisher; per field, where each value was found,
   every other value where the source disagrees with itself, or that the source gives
   none; a mandatory title, supplied where the source gives none; a Supplied value
   recorded as such with its procedure, that procedure's version and, for a model, its
   identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings. It names no tier, voter,
   selector or status word. It is internally consistent: "its title" is unconditional
   and the second sentence provides it; "author" is lower case wherever the value is
   supplied; the kept value is stated to be the item's, so one Source publication time
   per item remains for UIX-05 and UIX-06.
2. *The last sentence.* Requirement-level and verifiable: the version rule can be
   checked by comparing releases, and the recorded fields by inspecting a record. It
   mirrors the Method entry (model's identity, prompt, settings; versioned) without
   making the procedure a Method, so EVD-01, EVD-02, GOV-06, GOV-18 and D-002 do not
   reach it, as the Why states plainly. It does not break ACQ-07 or ACQ-08: the same
   phrase, "the wording of its prompt", sits in the version rule, where it can only
   mean wording that is the same for every item (a wording holding item text would
   change the version on every item), so the recorded wording holds no extracted text
   to survive an erasure.
3. *Complete.* No row that reads the title (ACQ-06, ACQ-07), the source (ACQ-05,
   OPS-27, OPS-28, UIX-05), the author (none besides this row) or the Source
   publication time (ACQ-01, UIX-05, UIX-06) is broken. [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] is
   unchanged.
4. *Conflicts.* None.

**[[ACQ-02]], the rationale.** The quote is exact. Each sentence matches the new
requirement. "Kept as the source's only where it gives them" is consistent with the
later sentence on supplied values: a value is recorded as the source's only where the
source gives it, and otherwise as supplied. No conflict.

**[[A-terms]] A2, Source publication time (four entries).** The quote is exact. Author
and Item publisher exclude each other in both directions; Supplied value and the last
sentences of Author and Source publication time agree; a fallback Item publisher is
not a Supplied value, as the Why says. The fallback, "the item's source as its Article
Record keeps it", uses "source" as the rows do (the Source Set entry the item was
collected from: ACQ-05, OPS-27, UIX-05) and states the ruling "The record's source
field" as given. CTX-01, CTX-09, CTX-12 and D-003 keep "publisher" for a corpus and
are untouched. No conflict.

**[[A-terms]] A2, Judgement.** The quote is exact. The added sentence states both
rulings ("Not a Judgement", for a supplied value and for the choice among disagreeing
values). The closed list is unchanged, so FBK-01, FBK-17, FBK-20, FBK-23, EVD-03, the
A1 Curator entry and D-002 read as before. No conflict.

**The Why and the rulings.** Each ruling it relies on is in the rulings file and is
stated as given: "A and B together"; "Site = publisher; joint lists are authors";
"Item publisher"; "Every conflicting value"; "The record's source field"; "No limit in
the requirement"; "Title stays mandatory"; a shown but undeclared date is BEACON's
inference; "Not a Judgement" twice; "option 3 with the question from option 1"
([[Q-025]] carries that question). Its figures match [[X-006]] (five of six items; 49
and 7 of 58; 26 organisations; no empty title). "How the earlier forms' gaps are met"
is true of this text, item by item.

**Observations for the owner to weigh; none is a gap.**
- The Why says the owner ruled "that ... the prompt be named by its wording". The
  ruling as recorded is: fix "its prompt", add settings and fix wording/clarity
  issues. Naming the prompt by its wording is this form's way of meeting it, not the
  owner's words. The Why would be exact as: "and then that settings be added and
  'its prompt' be fixed; this form names the prompt by its wording".
- That the recorded prompt holds no item text follows from the version rule by
  inference only, and the Why is not applied. One sentence in the rationale would
  state it: "The prompt's wording is the procedure's own, the same for every item,
  and holds none of the item's text (ACQ-07, ACQ-08)."
- Where the Item publisher is the fallback source, the Author entry's "an
  organisation other than the item's Item publisher" compares a credited organisation
  with a Source Set entry, and no row gives an entry a name. Whether a credited
  organisation is the source is left to the design ([[X-006]] decides it by equality
  with the source's name or host label).
- A source that carries other organisations' items becomes the Item publisher of
  each that names none. That is the ruling as given.
- In the requirement, "source" is both a field of the record and what "gives" a
  value; so a fallback Item publisher is recorded as the source, with "the source
  gives none". The row as it stands already uses both senses.
- The rationale's "A source often omits its Authors" attaches Authors to a source;
  the Author entry attaches them to an item. "An item's Authors" would match.
- The proposal's own three notes for the assessor stand as it states them.
