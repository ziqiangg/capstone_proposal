# CP-009 Drift rows restructured: no content changes

## The change

Eight replacements, no new row and no parameter change. Replacements: the Drift flag line of
[[A-terms]] A2, replaced by three lines, Drift flag, Drift sign and Drift check; the Drift window
line of [[A-terms]] A2, one phrase; and the requirement sentence and rationale of each of
[[SRC-16]], [[SRC-20]] and [[SRC-21]]. Every other line of [[A-terms]], [[SRC-16]], [[SRC-20]]
and [[SRC-21]] is unchanged, each row's frontmatter, heading and **Verification** line included.
No parameter note changes: [[DRIFT_WINDOW]] stays linked from [[SRC-16]] and [[SRC-20]], and
[[DRIFT_ALPHA]] from [[SRC-16]].

**1. [[A-terms]] A2, the Drift flag line, as it stands:**


**3. [[SRC-16]], the requirement, as it stands:**

```
WHEN a Cycle closes, BEACON shall raise, for each source enabled both when that Cycle started and when it closes, a Drift flag on the version of the source's Source Set entry then in force, for each thing a Drift flag may name, other than by one page contradicting it or one Collection meeting it (SRC-20, SRC-21), in which what BEACON read, recorded or met over that version's Drift window for that thing ([[DRIFT_WINDOW]]) departs from its Drift reference by more than chance alone would give with a probability of [[DRIFT_ALPHA]], unless a Drift flag on that version for that thing is open.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
WHEN a Cycle closes, BEACON shall raise, for each source's Drift check for that Cycle, a Drift flag on the check's version for each thing the check covers, other than by one page contradicting it or one Collection meeting it (SRC-20, SRC-21), in which what BEACON read, recorded or met over that version's Drift window for that thing ([[DRIFT_WINDOW]]) departs from its Drift reference by more than chance alone would give with a probability of [[DRIFT_ALPHA]].
```

**4. [[SRC-16]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** A source's pages change without notice: a redesign moves the byline, a bot block serves a page with no article on it. Collections still complete, so no failure is recorded (SRC-12), yet what BEACON reads is wrong. Each version is judged against its own Drift reference, of which how often something the entry holds finds nothing is part, not a departure in itself; judged as a rate, an occasional page with no article on it raises no flag where a rise does. The judgement runs when a Cycle closes (OPS-16), since a flag reaches the Curator only through the daily digest (A-terms, Digest), and leaves out a source disabled by then, disabling being already an answer to a flag (SRC-01). Collections and records go on under a flagged version, each record naming its version (ACQ-36). How a departure is judged is fixed by design.
```

**Replaced by:**

```
**Rationale.** A source's pages change without notice, as when a redesign moves the byline. Collections still complete, so no failure is recorded (SRC-12), yet what BEACON reads is wrong. Each version is judged against its own Drift reference, and as a rate, so an occasional page with no article on it raises no flag where a rise does. Flags are raised when a Cycle closes (OPS-16), as a flag reaches the Curator only through the daily digest (A-terms, Digest), and not for a source disabled by then, disabling being already an answer (SRC-01). How a departure is judged is fixed by design.
```

**6. [[SRC-20]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** A contradiction, a page showing untrue something the entry holds about the source's pages, is not produced by chance, so one raises a flag without a rate. It is looked for in the Drift window SRC-16 judges most things over, so what is judged is fixed at the close. After a Curator keeps the version despite a flag for that thing, the next page that contradicts it is not news, so how often the pages contradict it is then judged as a rate (SRC-16; A-terms, Drift flag). The judgement runs for the same sources, at the same point and on the same version as SRC-16's, for the same reasons.
```

**8. [[SRC-21]], the rationale, as it stands:**

```
**Rationale.** Three things a source's Collection can meet as a whole leave no item to judge and give no baseline for a rate: a listing with no entries; a listing whose first page is answered only with pages that show nothing of where the listing is, such as a bot wall's, which stops the Collection before it lists anything (A-terms, Completed without error); and every listed item BEACON tried to fetch refused in every way. None makes a Failed item (ACQ-01, SRC-12), so each raises a flag on its first occurrence, as a contradiction does (SRC-20). An empty listing still shows where it is, so no Collection meets both; it is not news for a source whose listing was empty before its version was put into effect, but a bot wall always is. After a keep, how often the source's Collections meet the thing is judged as a rate (SRC-16; A-terms, Drift window). The judgement runs for the same sources, at the same point and on the same version as SRC-16's, for the same reasons.
```

**Replaced by:**

## Why

**The ruling (2026-10-06).** On [[CP-008]]'s lengths after its rework, the owner ruled "Assess
now, tidy later (Recommended)": [[CP-008]] was assessed as it stood, and afterwards one small
restructure proposal for the drift rows only, their shared conditions into an A-terms line and
the Drift flag line's list of the things a flag names into its own line, touching [[SRC-16]],
[[SRC-20]] and [[SRC-21]] together. After [[CP-008]] was applied the owner ruled "continue small
restructural proposal". This is that proposal.

**It changes no content.** Only the form changes, as [[CP-006]]'s restructure ("Restructure
first", 2026-10-05) and [[CP-002]]'s did: every case keeps the outcome it has at HEAD (the case
check below). Why: the three rows each repeated, inline, when they are judged, for which sources,
on which version, after a keep, after a re-enable and while a flag is open, so [[SRC-21]]'s
sentence ran to 175 words, its rationale to 174 and the Drift flag line to 314, against rows such
as [[SRC-01]], [[SRC-12]] and [[ACQ-29]] of one sentence and a short reason. Repeated conditions
are also where the three rows can drift apart from each other at the next change.

**What moves where.**

- *The shared conditions* go into a new A2 line, **Drift check**, a definition: for a source
  enabled both when a Cycle started and when it closes, the version of the source's Source Set
  entry in force at that close and the things on it the check covers. It covers each thing a Drift
  flag may name, in each way its Drift sign gives, except three: a thing whose flag on that version
  is open; a thing by one page contradicting it or one Collection meeting it, where a Curator has
  kept that version despite a flag for that thing; and a thing by one Collection meeting it, where
  that Collection began before a Curator last enabled the source again. Each row keeps HEAD's
  trigger, "WHEN a Cycle closes", and its response names the sources, the version and the things:
  "for each source's Drift check for that Cycle, a Drift flag on the check's version for each
  thing the check covers" (in [[SRC-20]] "covers by one page contradicting it", in [[SRC-21]]
  "covers by that Collection meeting it"). The conditions sit in the definition, the obligation
  in the row.
- *The list of things a flag may name* moves, verbatim, from the Drift flag line into a new A2
  line, **Drift sign**, with one label added: the three whole-Collection things are each a
  *whole-Collection event*, the name [[CP-006]] and [[CP-008]] used for them. The label is needed
  because [[SRC-21]] no longer lists the three things, and the Drift window line and [[SRC-21]] must
  name them whichever way they are being judged, by one Collection before a keep or as a rate
  after it. The Drift flag line keeps its definition and says the thing it names is a Drift sign.
- *The Drift window line* named the three things as "a thing SRC-21 names"; it now names them
  "a whole-Collection event", pointing at the line that holds them. Nothing else in it changes.
- *Each rationale keeps its reason.* The case reasoning taken out is kept in the notes below.

**Two scopes kept narrow on purpose.** At HEAD the keep condition ("for which no Curator has kept
that version despite a Drift flag (SRC-18)") is in [[SRC-20]] and [[SRC-21]] only, and the
re-enable exclusion ("unless it began before a Curator last enabled the source again after
disabling it") in [[SRC-21]] only. [[SRC-16]] has neither: after a keep it is [[SRC-16]] that judges
the thing as a rate, and its Drift window already leaves out what came before a re-enable or a
keep. So the Drift check line scopes the keep exception to a thing "by one page contradicting it or
one Collection meeting it", and the re-enable exception to a thing "by one Collection meeting
it"; [[SRC-16]] takes the things the check covers "other than by one page contradicting it or one
Collection meeting it", so of the three exceptions only the open one reaches it, as at HEAD. Stated without those scopes, the re-enable exclusion would also have left out of
[[SRC-16]]'s window an item first tried, after a re-enable, in a Collection that began before it,
which the window counts at HEAD.

**One reading made explicit.** [[SRC-20]] and [[SRC-21]] say "for which no Curator has kept that
version despite a Drift flag (SRC-18)"; the Drift check line says "despite a Drift flag for that
thing". That is how the rows read at HEAD: the thing is what "for which" qualifies, and the Drift
flag line as it stands says the switch to a rate comes "once a Curator has kept the version despite
a flag for it", as [[SRC-18]]'s rationale does ("judged for what the flag named only on what came
since the keep").

**Reworked after the first assessment.** Two gaps were found. (1) The rows' trigger "WHEN BEACON
makes a source's Drift check for a Cycle" was keyed on BEACON's own act, which no row obliged, so
a BEACON making no check met all three rows. Each row now keeps HEAD's trigger, "WHEN a Cycle
closes", and raises "for each source's Drift check for that Cycle": a Drift check exists, by
definition, for every source enabled both when that Cycle started and when it closes, so each row
again obliges a judgement at every close for every such source, on the version in force at the
close. (2) The Drift check line's second and third sentences said what BEACON raises and judges,
obligations in an annex overriding the rows' "shall raise". The line is now a definition of what
a check covers, its three exceptions being HEAD's open, keep and re-enable conditions, and each
row raises a flag only "for each thing the check covers". The Drift flag, Drift sign and Drift
window lines and the three rationales other than [[SRC-16]]'s are as first proposed; [[SRC-16]]'s
rationale says "Flags are raised when a Cycle closes" where it said "The check runs", a check no
longer being an act.

**Case reasoning moved out of the rationales (notes, not applied).**

- [[SRC-16]]: "a bot block serves a page with no article on it" (the example of a change without
  notice; the rate sentence still names "an occasional page with no article on it"); "of which how
  often something the entry holds finds nothing is part, not a departure in itself" (the Drift
  reference is what the version gave, its rate of finding nothing included, so finding nothing at
  that rate departs from nothing); "Collections and records go on under a flagged version, each
  record naming its version (ACQ-36)" (no row stops a Collection for a flag; [[ACQ-36]] records the
  version).
- [[SRC-20]]: "The judgement runs for the same sources, at the same point and on the same version
  as SRC-16's, for the same reasons." Each row's trigger and response with the Drift check line now
  give all three, and [[SRC-16]]'s rationale the reasons.
- [[SRC-21]]: the bot wall "stops the Collection before it lists anything (A-terms, Completed
  without error)"; "An empty listing still shows where it is, so no Collection meets both" (it
  follows from the two events' wording in the Drift sign line); "After a keep, how often the
  source's Collections meet the thing is judged as a rate (SRC-16; A-terms, Drift window)" (the
  Drift sign line, the Drift check line's keep condition and [[SRC-16]]); and the same-sources
  sentence, as for [[SRC-20]].
