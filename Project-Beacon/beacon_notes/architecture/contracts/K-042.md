# K-042 Keep

## Shape
Undesigned: nothing blocks it yet but the design work; what does is asked when it begins. Expected to hold: the kind, a failing source (SRC-15) or a version kept despite an open Drift flag (SRC-18); the source; for the latter, the version, the flag and the thing it names; the Curator, the time and the reason. Intra-component: it restarts a source's failure count and a version's Drift reference and window for the thing kept.

## Invariants
Append-only. A keep names a source at its limit or an open flag when made.

## Failure
A keep missing its Curator or reason is never written.
