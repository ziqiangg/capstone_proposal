# K-039 Source failure state

## Shape
Undesigned: nothing blocks it yet but the design work; what does is asked when it begins. Expected to hold: per source, the count of Failed items since the reset point (SRC-12), the reset point and what set it, whether and when SOURCE_FAILURE_LIMIT was reached, the Failed items with their failure reasons (SRC-14), and the keeps (SRC-15). The Operator notice (OPS-21) reads the limit reached from it; who sends that notice is not yet a component.

## Invariants
Each item is counted once per reset. The limit is marked reached once per reset. A Drift flag or a silence adds nothing to the count.

## Failure
A count that could not be formed in full is marked incomplete with its reason, never reported as lower.
