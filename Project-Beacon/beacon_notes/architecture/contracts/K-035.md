# K-035 Erasure entry and log

## Shape
Undesigned: nothing blocks it yet but the design work; what does is asked when it begins. Expected to hold: per erasure or lift, in the order made, the item, who, when, why, its kind (a Curator's erasure under ACQ-08, a text erasure under ACQ-07, or a lift under ACQ-23), and what was removed. Source watch, not yet a C- note, will consume it too (its Drift window leaves out Erased items and handles lifts), and later Stages, so they are named here and not in consumers; Digests read it for ACQ-26.

## Invariants
Append-only, in the order made, so a restore can make each again in that order (ACQ-21). An entry is written only once what it removes is removed from the record.

## Failure
Undesigned.
