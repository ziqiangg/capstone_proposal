# K-006 Onboarding outcome or failure report

## Shape
Undesigned: nothing blocks it yet but the design work; what does is asked when it begins. Expected to hold: Who or what started it, items sampled, each part considered with its Standing (SRC-27), and a refusal with its grounds or a proposed version; or, for an Onboarding BEACON's own failure ended past its bound, the link and the reason (SRC-35). Views reads it for the Curator's and the Operator's read models.

## Invariants
An outcome rests on an Onboarding that finished looking; a failure report is never a refusal.

## Failure
A failure report is made once per Onboarding.
