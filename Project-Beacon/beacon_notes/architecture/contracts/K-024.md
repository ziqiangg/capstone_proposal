# K-024 Listing entry

## Shape
Undesigned: nothing blocks it yet but the design work; what does is asked when it begins. Expected to hold: Within collection. The address as given and made absolute; the route kind; the listing's title and date verbatim, with any machine-readable value and its precision; the source; the entry's own markup and the listing it came from.

## Invariants
Nothing is parsed out of free text. No entry is dropped for having no date. A sitemap's modification date is never a publication date.

## Failure
Undesigned.
