# S-014 Identify

## What it does
Gives each listing entry its key and usable aliases, applies both alias guards, looks the item up among the held items, and decides whether it is fetched: a new item, a pending one within its bound, or a held one with a listing signal. An item tried in one Cycle and in LISTED_ITEM_RETRY_CYCLES later Cycles without a fetch is not tried again (ACQ-39).

## Expects
K-024 Listing entry. K-007 Source Set entry version: the aliases the entry states. K-015 Held view: held items, their aliases and listed addresses, their erasure kind. K-020 Collection log and health counts: the earlier Cycles' tries of each listed item and their outcomes.

## Produces
K-025 Item reference: the key, aliases, listing entry, what is held of it, the fetch decision and the Cycles tried without a fetch.

## Done when
Every listing entry has one Item reference with a fetch decision, and none past its bound is decided for a fetch.

## Failure
A Held view or Collection log that cannot be read fetches nothing, with "held-item lookup failed"; an item is never treated as new because the lookup failed.
