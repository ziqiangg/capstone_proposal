# S-031 Erase and lift

## What it does
Erases and lifts. A Curator's erasure, with Curator, time and reason (ACQ-08), removes the text of every version and of any pending return (ACQ-17), every raw page recorded or pending and every value kept apart under ACQ-12 (ACQ-18), and leaves the pending return's record write for S-027 to complete (ACQ-41, ACQ-42); the item is then an Erased item, held or not (ACQ-22). An erasure under ACQ-07, once EXCLUDED_TEXT_RETENTION has passed since an OUT scope decision or a drop, removes the text alone (ACQ-17). Every Copy outside a backup is removed by the end of the first Cycle ending after the erasure, within 24 hours (ACQ-19); each backup is deleted by its stated expiry (ACQ-20); a restore makes again, in order, every erasure and lift made after its backup (ACQ-21). A Curator's lift, with Curator, time and reason (ACQ-23), restores nothing (ACQ-24). The scope decision and the drop come from components not yet in the vault; the Curator's request reaches it through views.

## Expects
K-032 Article Record version: the versions whose text is removed. K-022 Kept raw set: the raw pages removed. K-036 Pending return: what a return left unrecorded, removed and handed back for completion.

## Produces
K-035 Erasure entry and log: one entry per erasure or lift, in order.

## Done when
After an erasure no text, raw page or value it removes is readable in the record, in a pending return or, after the bound, in any Copy outside a backup, and the log holds every erasure and lift in the order made.

## Failure
An erasure that cannot remove everything it must is not logged as made; it is reported and retried, and the item is held as erased meanwhile. An ACQ-07 erasure whose scope decision or drop time is unknown is not made; neither is assumed.
