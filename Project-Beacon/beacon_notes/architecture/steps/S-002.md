# S-002 Test acceptance

## What it does
Probes the link through collection's fetch chain and either refuses it, naming each Refusal ground observed, marks it not tested, or lets it go on with cautions such as too few items, items already held, or another language.

## Expects
K-001 Candidate, for the link. K-013 Fetched item, from the probe, saying what was and was not obtained. K-015 Held view, to tell which listed items BEACON already holds.

## Produces
K-002 Acceptance: a refusal with its grounds, or leave to go on with its cautions, and which required parts were Tried.

## Done when
Each link that finished looking gives exactly one of refusal or leave to go on, and a refusal names only observed grounds for parts Tried.

## Failure
What looks like not reachable but may be BEACON's own crash or lost connection is a failure, not a ground: the run ends and Take a link starts it again (SRC-34). A Held view that cannot be read gives no caution, and says so; it is never read as nothing held.
