# S-001 Take a link

## What it does
Takes a link from a Curator, or a candidate source BEACON's search found, and starts an Onboarding of it, recording who or what started it, when and the reason given. It is also where an Onboarding that BEACON's own failure ended starts again from the same link, up to a bound fixed by design.

## Expects
Nothing from another step: a Curator's link and reason, or a candidate from the deferred search.

## Produces
K-001 Candidate: the link, who or what started the Onboarding, the time, the reason, and which start of it this is against the bound.

## Done when
Every Onboarding begins with a recorded Candidate, and none begins from anything other than a Curator's link or BEACON's own candidate (SRC-23).

## Failure
A link that is missing or not a URL starts no Onboarding and is returned to the Curator. A run ended by BEACON's own failure at any later step comes back here and starts again; when the bound is spent, Ask the Curator writes the failure report.
