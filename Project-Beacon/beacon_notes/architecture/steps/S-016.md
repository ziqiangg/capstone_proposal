# S-016 Render

## What it does
Renders an item's page only where the entry version's render policy says it is needed, keeping the browser's serialised document as one more labelled piece, and completes the Fetched item. A render obeys robots rules, the per-host pace and the deadline as a fetch does. It keeps no image bytes.

## Expects
K-026 Fetched pages. K-007 Source Set entry version: the render policy.

## Produces
K-013 Fetched item: labelled pieces with their URLs, the collection time, what was not obtained and why, and a fetch id. Onboarding's probe reaches it through the same chain.

## Done when
Every item with Fetched pages has one Fetched item, rendered where the policy requires, and stating where a render was needed and not obtained.

## Failure
A render that fails or reaches the deadline leaves the rendered piece not obtained, with the reason; the fetched pieces still pass on.
