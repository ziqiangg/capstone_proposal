# S-022 Credit the Item publisher

## What it does
Tells the Item publisher apart from the Authors: an organisation the item names as publishing it is a candidate for the Item publisher, never an Author. Where the item credits as its author its Item publisher, or its source where the source stands in place of its Item publisher, it records that it does and removes that credit from the Author candidates (ACQ-02).

## Expects
K-028 Normalised values for the Item publisher and the Authors. K-007 Source Set entry version: the source's name, to recognise a credit to the source.

## Produces
K-029 Publisher credit: the Item publisher candidates; the Author candidates with every credit to the Item publisher or the source removed; whether the item credits its Item publisher or its source, and where.

## Done when
No credit to the Item publisher or to the source remains among the Author candidates, and each one removed is recorded as a credit.

## Failure
A credit that cannot be told apart from an Author without guessing is held as found with that reason, and is kept neither as an Author nor as a credit.
