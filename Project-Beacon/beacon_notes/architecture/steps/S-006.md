# S-006 Ask the Curator

## What it does
Shows a Curator each open part on the check pages by the written rule, through the step-B form, an adapter behind a port a later screen can replace; records each answer (right, wrong, none, cannot tell) with who, when and, for a rejection, why; and assembles the Onboarding outcome. Where a run has failed every start the bound allows, it writes the failure report in place of an outcome.

## Expects
K-001 Candidate, for who started it and the link. K-002 Acceptance, for a refusal or the cautions. K-005 Checked part.

## Produces
K-006 Onboarding outcome or failure report: who or what started it, items sampled, each part considered with its Standing, and a refusal with its grounds or a proposed version; or the link and the reason BEACON failed.

## Done when
Every Onboarding that finished looking has one outcome shown to a Curator with every part's Standing (SRC-27), and every one ended by failure past the bound has one failure report, made once (SRC-35).

## Failure
A part the Curator answers in none of the ways SRC-24 names keeps its Standing. A rejected required part gives the sixth Refusal ground. An unanswered form leaves the outcome unaccepted; it never defaults an answer.
