# S-004 Propose parts

## What it does
For each part kind, a code-steered loop on the derive pages proposes a part, applies it through reading, and narrows it by its wrong matches, stopping when no narrowing is left. Parts a person wrote by hand enter here as that person's proposals. A name the same on every page is proposed as the site's own only where the pages declare it so; otherwise it is left for the Curator.

## Expects
K-003 Sample, its derive pages only. K-011 Part result, for each trial application of a part.

## Produces
K-004 Proposed part: per part kind, the proposal or none, its origin (BEACON or a person), and the trials that led to it.

## Done when
Every part kind the entry needs has a proposal or a recorded none, each read from derive pages only, and a body part holds the share SRC-32 sets of each page's varying prose.

## Failure
A Part result that could not be evaluated is never taken as matched nothing; the loop stops with no proposal for that kind, and says why. BEACON's own failure ends the run (SRC-34).
