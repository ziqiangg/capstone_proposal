# S-038 Keep despite

## What it does
Records a Curator's keep: of a source that has reached SOURCE_FAILURE_LIMIT (SRC-15), or of a version of a source's Source Set entry despite what an open Drift flag on it names (SRC-18), with the Curator, the time and the reason. A keep of a failing source restarts its count; a keep of a flagged version closes the flag and restarts that version's Drift reference and window for what the flag named. The Curator's means to keep is an adapter on the views side.

## Expects
K-039 Source failure state: that the source has reached the limit. K-016 Drift flag: that the flag is open, its version and the thing it names.

## Produces
K-042 Keep: its kind (failing source or flagged version), the source, the version and thing where flagged, the Curator, the time and the reason.

## Done when
Every keep a Curator makes is recorded once, and only against a source at its limit or an open flag.

## Failure
A keep without a Curator or a reason, or not against a source at its limit or an open flag, is refused and not recorded.
