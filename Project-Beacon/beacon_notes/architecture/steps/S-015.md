# S-015 Fetch

## What it does
Fetches each item decided for a fetch at its configured addresses, only in the ways of fetching the entry version names and in its order (SRC-30), within robots rules, a per-host pace, a fixed number of retries and the Collection's deadline (OPS-15). A page that shows none of what the entry holds as appearing on every page is not a fetch. An item whose content cannot be fetched is not returned (ACQ-01) and stays pending within its bound (ACQ-39).

## Expects
K-023 Collection plan: the deadline. K-025 Item reference: the decision and addresses. K-007 Source Set entry version: the ways of fetching, their order, and what appears on every page.

## Produces
K-026 Fetched pages: the pages as fetched, bytes unaltered, each with its status, way and attempts, or why the item was not obtained.

## Done when
Every item decided for a fetch has Fetched pages or a stated reason it was not obtained, and no way outside the entry's was tried.

## Failure
Each failure has its kind: disallowed, ended, refused by every way, not the template, timed out, deadline, not attempted, past the bound. None is retried beyond the fixed count, and none is a Failed item (ACQ-01).
