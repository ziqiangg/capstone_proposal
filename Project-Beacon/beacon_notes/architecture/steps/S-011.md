# S-011 Extract text

## What it does
Extracts an item's text from one fetched item, or from the kept pieces of one at a Re-extraction. A page runs the body ladder: the configured body part through Apply an Entry part, then the page's landmark, then one generic extractor, then the document body less its navigation and footer. It finds the headline above the body and runs the text from it. A document goes by its own path, chosen by its format's signature or declared media type: a text layer is converted; no text layer gives empty text with that reason; a text layer that cannot be read is rejected as an unreadable text layer and is never passed as text (ACQ-16). It fetches and renders nothing (ACQ-03).

## Expects
K-007 Source Set entry version: the body part and what the headline leaves out as furniture. K-013 Fetched item, or the kept pieces of one in the same form, with what was not obtained. K-011 Part result for the configured body part, from Apply an Entry part.

## Produces
K-012 Extraction: the text; the headline and whether finding it used another value; the piece and region the text came from; the reason where the text is empty or from another form (ACQ-15); the extraction procedure's version (ACQ-36, D-004).

## Done when
Every item given yields one Extraction carrying the procedure version, and every empty or substitute text carries its reason.

## Failure
A part not obtained, or text that cannot be derived from what is given, gives empty text with that reason (ACQ-15); nothing is fetched to fill it. No text layer, or an unreadable one, gives only text from another form the item offers, or none, with the reason (ACQ-16). A missing body part result moves the ladder to its next rung, never to empty text by default.
