# S-018 Log

## What it does
Writes the Collection log, append-only, its first line before any work: per item its outcome and each part's outcome and yield; per Collection whether it completed without error, or why not, including an end under OPS-15, so the time of the last Collection completed without error is known (SRC-04); the yield (SRC-05); every listed item tried, with its latest try's outcome (ACQ-39); and the raw health counts. It writes counts, not verdicts.

## Expects
K-023 Collection plan. K-024 Listing entry. K-025 Item reference. K-011 Part result. K-012 Extraction: empty text and its reason. K-013 Fetched item: what was not obtained. K-018 Item return: its kind.

## Produces
K-020 Collection log and health counts.

## Done when
Every listed item has one outcome line, and every Collection has one completion line with its reason.

## Failure
A log that cannot be written stops the Collection, which then has not completed without error.
