# C-05 Page reading

## Responsibility
Applies an Entry part to a page, giving found, matched nothing or could not be evaluated, below both onboarding and collection, so neither reaches into the other to read a page. Runs the body ladder, the headline and the text; reads documents by their own path; rejects an unreadable text layer. It is pure: it fetches nothing, renders nothing, reads no store and no clock, and writes nothing, so the same entry points serve onboarding, a Cycle, a Re-extraction and replay alike. Its one reason to change is the extraction procedure's version (ACQ-28).
