# C-04 Source onboarding and the Source Set

## Responsibility
Turns a link into a version of a Source Set entry, from intake to issue, the step-B form through which a Curator answers on open parts included; prepares a version correcting the one in force after Drift flags, recording each attempt; enables and disables sources; and records when each version comes into force and goes out of it. It reaches collection's fetch chain only through its probe port and watch's flags only through its flag port, both wired by the Cycle. SRC-07 and SRC-11, finding candidate sources, are carried here but deferred: a candidate enters at Take a link like a Curator's link. Its one reason to change is how a source's configuration is derived, checked and put into effect.
