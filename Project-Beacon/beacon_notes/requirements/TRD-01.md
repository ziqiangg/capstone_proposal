# TRD-01

WHEN a scheduled Cycle closes, BEACON shall identify the trends across the developments that have a contributing item discovered within the [[TREND_WINDOW]] ending at that Cycle.

**Rationale.** A single development does not show direction. Anchoring the window to the Cycle makes "Active trend" definable.
