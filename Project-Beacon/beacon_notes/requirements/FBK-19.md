# FBK-19

WHILE a Revised result awaits a Curator's approval, BEACON shall use the result it would replace, and not the Revised result, in selecting each Recommendation Set and in composing each Briefing and Delivery.

**Rationale.** A result that no person has checked must not decide what Readers are told, directly or through a ranking. Until the Curator approves it, BEACON works from the last approved result, so a development's place in a new Briefing is not moved by a value no Reader can see.
