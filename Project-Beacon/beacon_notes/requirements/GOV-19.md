# GOV-19

WHEN [[PERSONAL_DATA_RETENTION]] has passed since a user was deactivated, BEACON shall erase that user's contact details, keeping the user's name in each record that attributes an action to that user.

**Rationale.** Who corrected, withdrew or approved something stays part of the evidence (D, GOV-16). Contact details do not.
