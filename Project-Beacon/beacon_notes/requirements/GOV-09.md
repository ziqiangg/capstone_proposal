# GOV-09

BEACON shall accept through a delivery channel only two actions: a Reader's start of the bot through a link that BEACON issued to that Reader, and an opt-out from a chat account linked to a current recipient.

**Rationale.** Opting in is completed, and opting out may be done, through the channel; nothing else a chat account sends reaches a capability, so the channel cannot be used to act as a user.
