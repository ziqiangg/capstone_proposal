# DEV-13

WHEN a Curator merges or splits developments, BEACON shall re-derive, for each resulting development, its claim alignment and agreement, its assessment and its mappings.

**Rationale.** A development's other Judgements rest on its membership. After a membership correction they are stale until re-derived. Where the development is Published, FBK-20 routes the change through the correction route, so Readers see only the Curator's corrected Account.
