# DLV-22

WHEN a Curator corrects a Published development that a Delivery already sent contained, BEACON shall send a correction notice for that development to each recipient who received that Delivery, without waiting for the next Delivery, within the limit DLV-27 sets.

**Rationale.** A recipient who may have acted on a wrong statement must learn of the correction promptly, not at the next Delivery. The notice follows from the Curator's one correcting action, so no separate step can forget it.
