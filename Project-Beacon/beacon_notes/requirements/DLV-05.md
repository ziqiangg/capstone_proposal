# DLV-05

A recipient shall stop being one WHEN they opt out, in BEACON or through the delivery channel; WHEN they cease to hold the Reader role; or WHEN their account is deactivated.

**Rationale.** Deliveries end when consent or entitlement ends, with no one having to remember to remove the recipient.
