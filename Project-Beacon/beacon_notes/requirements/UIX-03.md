# UIX-03

BEACON shall provide a Reader with the means to browse Discovered items, developments, trends, mappings and Briefings.

**Rationale.** A Reader who cannot find a development once the Briefing has moved on falls back on the sources BEACON is meant to replace. A Recommendation Set is reached through its Briefing.
