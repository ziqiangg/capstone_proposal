# OUT-10

BEACON shall show a Reader no numeric confidence, no Method or Method version, no Baseline's capture date or version, and no version of the Scope Criteria or the assessment criteria, nor the date of one.

**Rationale.** A Reader needs to know how sure BEACON is and what a development bears on, not how BEACON is built: confidence is shown in words (CTX-06, ASM-05), and a mapping by its corpus and item identifier (CTX-06). The exact numbers, the Method versions, the Baselines and the criteria versions are defensibility data, kept in the decision record (EVD-02) for the non-Reader roles.
