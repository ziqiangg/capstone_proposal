# OUT-11

WHEN Materially new information joins a Published development, BEACON shall compose a new Account of that development.

**Rationale.** A Development record's Account should agree with the development's current evidence and Significance. Published Briefings keep the Account they were composed with (EVD-08 keeps the prior version).
