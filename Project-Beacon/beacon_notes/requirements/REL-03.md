# REL-03

BEACON shall pass every Admitted item, and no other Discovered item, to the Stages that follow the scope decision.

**Rationale.** A Discovered item that is not admitted, other than an Erased item BEACON does not hold, of which nothing is retained (ACQ-22, ACQ-42), is retained (ACQ-02) but not analysed, so a wrong OUT keeps it out of every development, assessment and Briefing until its scope is re-derived.
