# FBK-04

WHEN a Curator corrects a Published development, BEACON shall show Readers its corrected Account in place of the prior one, marked as corrected, wherever that development appears.

**Rationale.** The correction replaces what Readers were told, in past Briefings too, because the aim is to stop a wrong statement from being relied on; the mark shows that it changed. A correction is content, not a failure, so Readers see it, and the recipients who received the development get a correction notice. The same Curator action (FBK-16) records the corrected Judgements behind the Account (FBK-03). EVD-08 keeps the prior Account.
