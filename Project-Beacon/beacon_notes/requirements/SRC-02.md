# SRC-02

BEACON shall show a Curator every source in the Source Set, with its state (enabled or disabled), the time of its last successful Collection, its yield and its Health entry for the latest Closed Cycle that started while it was enabled.

**Rationale.** A source whose health is invisible decays unnoticed. SRC-04 and SRC-05 record the time and the yield shown; the Health entry shown is the one the Curator digest reports for that Cycle (SRC-19), kept in view between digests.
