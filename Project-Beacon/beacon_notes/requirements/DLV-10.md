# DLV-10

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to exclude a development from a Delivery not yet sent, and to reverse that exclusion, recording the Curator, the time and the reason.

**Rationale.** Editorial control has to exist for each development, or the whole channel gets suspended instead.
