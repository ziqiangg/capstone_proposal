# OUT-08

BEACON shall show a Curator, for each Briefing, its estimated reading time beside [[TARGET_READING_MINUTES]].

**Rationale.** The target is a policy the Curator tunes (through the bounds and the selection floor), so the comparison is the Curator's; a Reader gains nothing from it.
