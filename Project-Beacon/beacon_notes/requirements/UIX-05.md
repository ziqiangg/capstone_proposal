# UIX-05

BEACON shall provide a Reader with the means to filter Discovered items by source, item\_type, subject\_tags, collection time and Source publication time; developments by Significance, by the item\_type and subject\_tags of their contributing items, and by the time of their latest contributing item; trends by characterisation and period; mappings by corpus and framework item; and Briefings by date.

**Rationale.** Each filter applies only to the class that carries the attribute.
