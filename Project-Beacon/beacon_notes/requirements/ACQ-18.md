# ACQ-18

WHEN a Curator erases an item under ACQ-08, BEACON shall remove every raw page of the item, whether recorded in its Article Record or not, and every value kept for the item under ACQ-12, keeping the rest of its Article Record other than the extracted text that ACQ-17 removes.

**Rationale.** Split from ACQ-08. A request to remove an item reaches the page its source published, every part of it (A-terms, Raw page), as well as its text. The values ACQ-12 keeps apart are as they were delivered, may hold a person's name and are not the record's fields, so they are removed too. The fields and where each value was found stay, so that a reviewer can still see what the record said and where it came from.
