# DLV-29

WHEN a Curator restores a withdrawn development, BEACON shall send a restore notice for that development to each recipient who received a holding notice for it, without waiting for the next Delivery, within the limit DLV-27 sets.

**Rationale.** A recipient told that a development is under review must also learn that the review found nothing to correct; otherwise they go on treating a sound development as doubtful. The notice says only that the development is restored, unchanged.
