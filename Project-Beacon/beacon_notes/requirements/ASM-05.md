# ASM-05

BEACON shall show a Reader, for each assessment, its confidence stated in words and, for each criterion of the assessment criteria, the outcome and the Evidence locators it rests on.

**Rationale.** A ranking that cannot be explained is an opinion with a number attached. The Reader needs the basis and how sure BEACON is; the criteria version and its date, the numeric confidence and the Method version stay in the decision record (EVD-02), for the non-Reader roles.
