# DEV-05

BEACON shall record, for each group of Aligned claims, whether their sources agree or disagree, or UNKNOWN where agreement cannot be determined.

**Rationale.** Disagreement between sources on a fact must be surfaced, not silently resolved; OUT-02 puts it in the Account.
