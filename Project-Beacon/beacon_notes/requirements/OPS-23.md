# OPS-23

Each Operator digest shall report, for each Cycle closed since the previous digest, whether it was a Complete cycle, the sources not collected, its Unfinished and Failed items with their sources, the items BEACON dropped, and when its Briefing was Published or that the Cycle had nothing new, and each Delivery skipped since the previous digest with the reason.

**Rationale.** This is where what the Reader is never told is logged for the Operator, a day at a time.
