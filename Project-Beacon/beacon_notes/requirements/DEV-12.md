# DEV-12

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to split a development into two or more, assigning each of its contributing items to one or more of the resulting developments.

**Rationale.** DEV-01 holds one development per underlying event. When the grouping Method errs, the Curator restores that, as a correction of development membership.
