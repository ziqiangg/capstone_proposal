# TRD-02

BEACON shall characterise each identified trend as exactly one of emerging, recurring, intensifying, stable or declining, as A-terms defines them, or as UNKNOWN where the trend cannot be matched across windows.

**Rationale.** The characterisation tells the Reader whether a pattern is new, repeated, growing, steady or weakening. The definitions exclude one another. How to match a trend across windows is an open question, and until it is answered this row cannot be fully verified.
