# CTX-10

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to activate one Baseline of a corpus, in place of that corpus's active Baseline.

**Rationale.** A new capture never changes mappings by itself; the Curator decides adoption. Activating a Baseline is a trigger (GOV-06).
