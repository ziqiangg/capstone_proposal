# OUT-02

Each Account shall state what happened, why it matters within BEACON's subject area, the controls and threat-framework items it bears on or that none was mapped and why, each fact on which its sources disagree with what each side reports, and its Significance.

**Rationale.** These are what a Reader needs to act without opening anything else. Mapping may be declined (CTX-04), and the Account then says so rather than falls silent. Disagreement between sources is information the Reader must weigh, not something BEACON resolves out of sight (DEV-05).
