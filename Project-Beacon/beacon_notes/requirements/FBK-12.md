# FBK-12

WHEN re-derivation on a trigger in the Trigger Register changes a Judgement behind a development's Published Account, BEACON shall compose a revised Account of that development.

**Rationale.** A Published Account whose Judgements have changed no longer matches them; the revised Account is a Revised result, so no Reader sees it until a Curator approves it (FBK-17).
