# DLV-30

IF a development that is withdrawn would otherwise be among the developments of a Delivery, THEN BEACON shall fill its place with the next-ranked development that qualifies for that Delivery.

**Rationale.** A withdrawal stops a development from spreading; it should not also shorten the day's Delivery. The development is left out while it is withdrawn, and the next-ranked development takes its place, as it does when a Curator excludes one (DLV-11).
