# DLV-04

A Reader shall become a recipient only by opting in, in BEACON, to a statement that names BEACON and states what will be delivered and on what schedule, and then starting the bot through the link BEACON gives them for it.

**Rationale.** Consent must be informed and must come from the Reader: the statement says who, what and when, and the bot cannot message anyone who has not started it.
