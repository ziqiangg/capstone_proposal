# FBK-20

IF a Curator's correction of a Judgement would change what Readers see of a Published development, THEN BEACON shall accept that correction only as part of a correction of that development (FBK-16).

**Rationale.** A correction that silently changed what Readers see would leave them unaware that something they may have relied on was wrong. Routing it through the one correcting action means that the Account is marked corrected and that the recipients who received the development get a correction notice, whichever means the Curator started from. A correction that changes nothing Readers see needs neither.
