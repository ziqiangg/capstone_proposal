# UIX-06

BEACON shall provide a Reader with the means to sort developments by Significance, by number of distinct sources and by the time of their latest contributing item; Discovered items by collection time and Source publication time; trends by period; mappings by corpus and framework item; and Briefings by date.

**Rationale.** A Reader looking for what matters most, what is most widely reported or what is newest needs the order to match the question. Each sort applies only to the class that carries the attribute.
