# DLV-28

WHEN an Operator withdraws a development that a Delivery already sent contained, BEACON shall send a holding notice for that development to each recipient who received that Delivery, without waiting for the next Delivery, within the limit DLV-27 sets.

**Rationale.** A recipient who may act on the withdrawn development must learn at once that it is under review. The notice states no content judgement, so it follows from the Operator's withdrawal alone. If a restored development is withdrawn again on the same day, no second holding notice goes out that day (DLV-27), though the development is hidden again at once.
