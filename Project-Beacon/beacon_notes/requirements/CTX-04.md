# CTX-04

BEACON shall decide, for each development, whether mapping it serves one of the approved mapping purposes, before mapping it.

**Rationale.** A similarity score is not an approved purpose. The decision is part of the mapping Judgement, so EVD-01 records it, and a declined mapping is recorded as an outcome (EVD-06), not left as an absence. The approved mapping purposes are a Governed Artefact.
