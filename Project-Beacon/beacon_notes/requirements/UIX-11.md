# UIX-11

BEACON shall show a Reader, of an item's extracted text, only the passages that an Evidence locator or a search match resolves to, each with a link to the item at its source.

**Rationale.** A Reader needs the passage that supports a statement, and the source for the rest. Short passages with a link keep the Reader's attention on the evidence and send the Reader to the original for everything else.
