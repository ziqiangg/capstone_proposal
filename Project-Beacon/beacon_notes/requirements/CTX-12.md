# CTX-12

BEACON shall keep every Baseline it captures, unchanged, with its source, its capture time and the publisher's version where one exists.

**Rationale.** Each capture is an immutable Baseline. Keeping every one means that no mapping needs re-deriving merely to stay interpretable.
