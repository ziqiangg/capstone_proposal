# GOV-12

BEACON shall provide an Administrator with the means to grant any role to any user, the Administrator included, and to withdraw it.

**Rationale.** An Administrator may grant roles to themselves. Granting and withdrawing are one capability with its inverse. GOV-16 records each grant.
