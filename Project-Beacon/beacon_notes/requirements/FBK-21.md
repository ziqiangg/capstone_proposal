# FBK-21

BEACON shall not replace a Judgement that a Curator has corrected as a result of re-derivation on a trigger in the Trigger Register.

**Rationale.** A correction records a person's finding that BEACON got something wrong; a re-run under a new Method or new criteria could bring the error back without anyone deciding it. The corrected Judgement stays in force, and a re-derived result that differs from it goes to the Curator (FBK-22).
