# OPS-18

WHEN a scheduled Cycle closes with an Unfinished item or a Failed item, BEACON shall select that Cycle's Recommendation Set and compose its Briefing without that item's contribution.

**Rationale.** Late is better than never: after the grace period the day is Published with what finished, and the Reader is not told why.
