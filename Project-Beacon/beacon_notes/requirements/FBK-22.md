# FBK-22

IF re-derivation on a trigger in the Trigger Register produces, for a Judgement that a Curator has corrected, a result that differs from the corrected Judgement, THEN BEACON shall add that Judgement, with the corrected and the re-derived results, to the next Curator digest.

**Rationale.** A new Method or new criteria may agree with the correction or may not. The Curator decides whether the correction still stands, and can correct the Judgement again (FBK-01); the daily digest carries the question without an interruption. A re-derived result that agrees with the correction needs no one's attention.
