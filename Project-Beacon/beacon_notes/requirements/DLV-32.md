# DLV-32

WHEN [[CONSENT_RECORD_RETENTION]] has passed since a Reader's latest opt-out, BEACON shall erase that Reader's consent records.

**Rationale.** Consent records prove, for a period after opt-out, that every Delivery had consent (D). After that period they are personal data with no purpose.
