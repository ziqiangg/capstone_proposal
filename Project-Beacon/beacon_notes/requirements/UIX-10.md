# UIX-10

BEACON shall show a Curator, in one list, every vocabulary gap, every proposed subject vocabulary value, every proposed candidate source, every silent source, every source whose failures have reached its limit and that no Curator has since kept, disabled or removed, every re-derivation awaiting confirmation, every Revised result awaiting approval, every withdrawn development and every development whose Current assessment has a Significance of UNKNOWN.

**Rationale.** The Curator's side of CLS-02, CLS-05, SRC-06, SRC-07, of failing sources, of re-derivation, of withdrawals and of developments BEACON could not assess (ASM-09). Without one list, each is found only by looking.
