# OPS-26

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to restore a withdrawn development without changing it.

**Rationale.** A withdrawal may turn out to be unnecessary, and a development that needs no correction must be able to come back as it was; a correction brings it back corrected (FBK-04). Deciding that the withdrawn content was right is a content judgement, which is the Curator's, as the correction is; the Operator's brake needs none. The recipients who received a holding notice are told that it is restored.
