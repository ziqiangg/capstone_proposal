# ACQ-26

WHEN a Curator erases an item under ACQ-08, BEACON shall add to the next Curator digest every Judgement, and every Account of a Published development, whose evidence includes an Evidence locator into that item's Article Record.

**Rationale.** A passage that BEACON's own Judgement or Account quotes is evidence, not a Copy, so an erasure does not rewrite it: a silent change to what BEACON Published could not be traced. Each such locator now resolves to the time and reason of the erasure (EVD-03), so a Reader is no longer shown the passage (UIX-11) without the Account being edited. The Curator sees each one and decides whether it needs a correction or a withdrawal, under the rows that govern those.
