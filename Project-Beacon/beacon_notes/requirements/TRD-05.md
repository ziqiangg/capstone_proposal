# TRD-05

BEACON shall show, wherever the web interface shows a development, whether its Current assessment records it as Novel, whether or not it belongs to an Active trend.

**Rationale.** A development without precedent cannot yet be a trend, but it can be the most important thing that week. The mark is shown on every view of the development, including views of developments below the selection floor, where no Account exists; it does not bring a development into selection.
