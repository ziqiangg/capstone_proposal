# CLS-05

WHEN a chunk's subject matter fits no value in the subject vocabulary, BEACON shall propose a new subject vocabulary value to a Curator.

**Rationale.** The subject space grows with the field. A proposal lets it grow without an unreviewed value entering the vocabulary. A Curator may also add a value directly, and both kinds of change are attributed (GOV-07).
