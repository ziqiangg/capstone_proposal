# DEV-01

BEACON shall hold exactly one development for each underlying event, incident, vulnerability, announcement, research result or campaign that Admitted items report.

**Rationale.** Twelve outlets covering one breach are one thing to know about, not twelve. "Report" is the one membership criterion, and DEV-06 and DEV-07 share it. One item may belong to several developments, as a roundup does.
