# REL-04

WHEN the scope Method is evaluated against the labelled evaluation set, the proportion of that set's items to which the Method gives a scope\_decision of UNKNOWN shall be at or below [[MAX_ABSTENTION_RATE]].

**Rationale.** Without a ceiling, a Method satisfies REL-02 by abstaining on everything.
