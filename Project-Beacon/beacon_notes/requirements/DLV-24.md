# DLV-24

Each Delivery shall contain only developments that no earlier Delivery contained, unless Materially new information has joined them since.

**Rationale.** A development re-enters a Briefing only with Materially new information (SEL-08), and it is delivered again only then; without this row, a development that appears in two Briefings drawn on by one Delivery, or re-sent after a pause, would repeat. When nothing qualifies, DLV-20 sends nothing.
