# SRC-24

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to confirm or reject, on the source's pages BEACON shows for it, each Entry part of an Onboarding outcome whose Standing is Not checked, recording for a rejection the Curator, the time and the reason, and to confirm, for one Not established, that the source offers none of what it is for.

**Rationale.** BEACON establishes and checks what it can by itself; what it could not check goes to a person before the outcome is accepted, and that person is a Curator, the role that accepts it. A confirmation makes the part Checked, a rejection Not established, and a confirmation that the source offers none makes it None offered (A-terms, Standing); a part the Curator answers in none of these ways keeps its Standing. A refusal resting on a rejection names its recorded reason (A-terms, Refusal ground). The Curator answers on pages BEACON shows, so that the answer rests on the source's own pages.
