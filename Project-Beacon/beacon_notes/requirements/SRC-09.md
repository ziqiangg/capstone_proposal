# SRC-09

WHEN BEACON Publishes a statement about frequency, breadth, absence or direction across developments, a trend included, BEACON shall state beside it the period that statement rests on and the sources actually collected in that period.

**Rationale.** Such a statement describes only what BEACON read. Without the sources and the period stated, it reads as a claim about the world.
