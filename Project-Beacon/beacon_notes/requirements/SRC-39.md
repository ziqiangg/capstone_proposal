# SRC-39

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to see a version prepared under SRC-37, with each of its Entry parts and that part's Standing, and, where its Entry parts locating the listing, telling items' addresses apart and giving the ways of fetching are established, to accept it as correcting the version it was prepared from while that version is in force, putting it into effect.

**Rationale.** This acceptance is the correction a Drift flag asks for (SRC-17); it re-extracts the items collected under the faulty version (ACQ-29), which then read the accepted one (ACQ-32). Each part's checking is shown (SRC-27). A version lacking a required part is shown but cannot be accepted: an enabled source is collected each Cycle (SRC-10) in the ways its entry names (SRC-30), and one with no listing, addresses or ways to fetch would be a silent dead source, as such an Onboarding outcome is a refusal (A-terms, Onboarding outcome); its flags stay open. Who accepted, and when, is recorded (GOV-07).
