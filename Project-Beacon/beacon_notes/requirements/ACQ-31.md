# ACQ-31

BEACON shall provide the Operator with the means to make a Re-extraction of the items the Operator names that are not Erased items and whose Article Records keep a raw page.

**Rationale.** An item beyond a release's reach (ACQ-28) is otherwise re-extracted only when it returns or its source's entry is corrected; the Operator's command reaches any other, such as a source's older items after a release. Each Re-extraction it makes is bound as every other is: it reads only what the record retains (ACQ-03) under the entry version ACQ-32 sets, never reaches an Erased item (ACQ-35), and records what ACQ-09, ACQ-33, ACQ-34 and ACQ-36 say. The Re-extractions of one command are one trigger (GOV-06), counted once (ACQ-37).
