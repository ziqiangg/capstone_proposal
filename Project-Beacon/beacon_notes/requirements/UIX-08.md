# UIX-08

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to filter Discovered items by scope\_decision and by Scope Criteria version, and to see the Scope Criteria version of each.

**Rationale.** Examining the boundary of the scope decision across criteria versions is the Curator's work, since the Curator owns the Scope Criteria.
