# FBK-11

BEACON shall not change a Published Briefing as a result of re-derivation on a trigger in the Trigger Register.

**Rationale.** A Briefing is what BEACON told its Readers on that day. Rewriting it after a model or criteria change would make the record of what was said unreliable, and a change of Method is not a correction. A Curator's correction and an Operator's withdrawal are not re-derivation, so they still reach a Published Briefing.
