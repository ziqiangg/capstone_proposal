# OPS-25

WHILE a development is withdrawn, BEACON shall neither show it to a Reader nor include it in a Delivery.

**Rationale.** A withdrawal that left the development in a Briefing, on its record or in the next Delivery would not stop it spreading. A Briefing already Published keeps the development in its composition, but no Reader sees it there while it is withdrawn; selection leaves it out meanwhile, and a Delivery fills its place with the next-ranked development. The withdrawal ends when a Curator corrects the development or restores it.
