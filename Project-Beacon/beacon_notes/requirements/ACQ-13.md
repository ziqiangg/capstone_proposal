# ACQ-13

BEACON shall record in the item's Article Record a title, author or publication time that it supplies where the source states none that BEACON can keep as a Supplied value, never as stated by the source, with the procedure that supplied it and that procedure's version, which changes whenever the procedure, its model, the wording of its prompt or its settings change, and, where a model supplied it, the model's identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings.

**Rationale.** Split from ACQ-02. A value BEACON supplies is recorded as supplied, with the procedure and version that supplied it and, for a model, its identity, the wording of its prompt and its settings, so that it is never read as what the source said and can be traced to what produced it.
