# CTX-01

BEACON shall hold the SSP, with every control of its Cybersecurity and Digital Service Standards catalogs, its system-type profiles and its level definitions, as a Baseline.

**Rationale.** Readers see the full SSP: all 248 controls today, 156 Cybersecurity and 92 Digital Service Standards. The publisher gives no version number, so a Baseline's capture time and content identify it. The system-type profiles and levels let a Reader judge where a control applies; nothing is filtered by them.
