# DLV-13

BEACON shall keep a record of each opt-in and opt-out, with the Reader, the route, the time and the wording consented to, including after the Reader stops being a recipient, until DLV-32 erases it.

**Rationale.** Consent has to be demonstrable after the fact, including after the Reader has left.
