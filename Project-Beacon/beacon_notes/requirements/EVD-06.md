# EVD-06

IF BEACON cannot determine a Judgement, or declines or withholds it, THEN BEACON shall record UNKNOWN, declined or withheld, respectively, as that Judgement's outcome, with the reason.

**Rationale.** Collapsing missing evidence into a default silently manufactures a decision. A decline that leaves no trace looks the same as a Stage that never ran. The record is for the non-Reader roles; a Reader sees a declined mapping only as OUT-02's "none was mapped, and why".
