# DLV-33

BEACON shall provide a Reader with the means to opt in to Deliveries and to opt out of them.

**Rationale.** Consent is given and withdrawn by the Reader, in BEACON as well as through the channel (DLV-05, GOV-09).

**Verification.** none yet
