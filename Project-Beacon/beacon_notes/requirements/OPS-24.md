# OPS-24

BEACON shall provide an Operator with the means to withdraw a Published development at once, without changing its content.

**Rationale.** When BEACON has Published something wrong, someone must be able to stop it spreading at any hour. Withdrawing needs no judgement of the content, so the Operator, who runs BEACON, can act before a Curator is available; the Curator then corrects it.
