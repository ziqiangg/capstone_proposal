# FBK-16

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to correct a Published development in one action, by writing the corrected text of its Account and correcting each Judgement behind that Account that was wrong.

**Rationale.** When BEACON has told Readers something wrong, a person writes what should have been said; the Curator, who already corrects Judgements (FBK-01), holds the content judgement this needs. One action does the whole correction: the Account is replaced and marked corrected (FBK-04), the corrected Judgements are recorded, the originals kept, for later compositions to reuse (FBK-02, FBK-03), and the recipients who received the development get a correction notice. Nothing is re-derived, so the correction waits for no re-run of the analysis.
