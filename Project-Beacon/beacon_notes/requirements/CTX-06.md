# CTX-06

Each mapping that BEACON Publishes shall carry its Evidence locators, an explanation, its confidence stated in words, and the name of its corpus and the identifier of its framework item.

**Rationale.** A Reader needs to know which control or technique a development bears on (for example "ATT&CK T1190" or "SSP AC-3"), the evidence, the reasoning and how sure BEACON is. The Baseline the mapping was made against, with its capture date and version, stays in the decision record (EVD-02) with the Method version and the numeric confidence, for the non-Reader roles; that record keeps the mapping interpretable after the corpus changes.
