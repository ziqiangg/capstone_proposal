# OPS-02

BEACON shall record, for every Cycle, whether it is scheduled or exceptional, its start time, the time it closed, the sources not collected in it, whether or not it is a Complete cycle, and the time its Briefing was Published or that none was.

**Rationale.** Every operational view reads from this record. Completeness and lateness are recorded for the non-Reader roles; no Reader output states them.
