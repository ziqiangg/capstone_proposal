# OPS-09

BEACON shall provide an Operator with the means to pause and resume scheduled Cycles without suspending delivery.

**Rationale.** An intervention must not require stopping the service. Suspending delivery is a separate capability (OPS-11). While Cycles are paused, no Briefing is Published, so no Delivery is sent (DLV-08), and no Briefing is late (OPS-12).
