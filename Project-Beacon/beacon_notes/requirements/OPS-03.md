# OPS-03

BEACON shall record, for each Discovered item other than an Erased item that BEACON does not hold, the Stage it has reached and that Stage's state: waiting, in progress, complete or failed.

**Rationale.** Otherwise the first sign of a problem is a missing Briefing with no explanation. OPS-07 shows the record.
