# ACQ-19

WHEN BEACON removes anything under ACQ-17 or ACQ-18, BEACON shall remove every Copy of it that BEACON holds outside a backup, apart from a value that the item's Article Record still holds, by the end of the first Cycle that ends after the erasure is made, and in any case within 24 hours of it.

**Rationale.** An erasure that left the text or the values it removes searchable, or readable through a Chunk or anything else made from it, would leave the item in BEACON, and a source's request to remove it would not be met. A Copy is named by what it holds, not by how it is made, so a Stage designed later is bound without its design being named here. A value the record still keeps, such as the title, may still be shown wherever BEACON shows it. The end of the Cycle, and in any case 24 hours, bounds how long an erased passage can still be found, including while Cycles are paused; a passage BEACON's own evidence quotes is not a Copy and is flagged instead (ACQ-26).
