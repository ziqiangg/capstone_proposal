# FBK-09

WHEN a Curator confirms or narrows [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] for a trigger, BEACON shall re-derive every Judgement that the trigger affects within that horizon.

**Rationale.** Every re-derivation a trigger calls for happens, once the Curator has confirmed or narrowed its horizon. What the re-derived Judgements change reaches Readers, and selection, only once a Curator approves it (FBK-12 to FBK-14, FBK-17, FBK-19), and a Judgement a Curator has corrected stays in force (FBK-21).
