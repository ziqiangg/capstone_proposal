# ACQ-23

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to lift the erasure of an Erased item, recording the Curator, the time and the reason.

**Rationale.** Without a lift, a mistaken erasure, or one whose source withdraws its request, would leave an item that BEACON can never keep again except by editing its stores by hand. A lift is recorded as an erasure is (ACQ-08), so the record shows who lifted it, when and why.
