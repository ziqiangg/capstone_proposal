# OPS-17

BEACON shall not Publish a scheduled Cycle's Briefing before that Cycle has closed.

**Rationale.** A Briefing Published while reachable items are still being processed leaves out what BEACON could have told the Reader that day. OPS-16 bounds the wait with a grace period.
