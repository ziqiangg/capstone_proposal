# SEL-04

BEACON shall include in a Recommendation Set only developments with at least one contributing item whose item\_type includes a genre in [[ELIGIBLE_GENRES]].

**Rationale.** The Curator decides which genres merit a Reader's attention. A development qualifies through any one of its contributing items, since one development is often reported in several genres.
