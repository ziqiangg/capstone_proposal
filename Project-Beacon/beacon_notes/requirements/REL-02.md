# REL-02

IF the confidence of a scope decision is below the floor that the scope Method in force sets, THEN BEACON shall record that scope\_decision as UNKNOWN.

**Rationale.** Missing a relevant item costs more than admitting an irrelevant one. An UNKNOWN item is an Admitted item, so doubt resolves toward analysis.
