# DLV-18

BEACON shall provide a Reader with the means to re-link their Deliveries to a chat account, a new one or the same one again.

**Rationale.** A Reader who changes chat account, or whose Deliveries were paused (DLV-17), resumes by re-linking, without having to opt out and opt in again. The new link is made as the first one was (DLV-26).

**Verification.** none yet
