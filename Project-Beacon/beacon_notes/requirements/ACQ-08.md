# ACQ-08

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to erase an item, recording the Curator, the time and the reason.

**Rationale.** A source may ask for its text to be removed, and whether to do so is the Curator's decision; the erasure should not need a release. The record shows who erased what, when and why.
