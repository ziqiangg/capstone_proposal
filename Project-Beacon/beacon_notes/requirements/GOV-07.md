# GOV-07

WHEN a Governed Artefact changes, BEACON shall keep the prior version and record the new version with its time and who or what made the change: the user, or the release of BEACON that made it.

**Rationale.** A change without attribution cannot be reviewed. A change can come from a Curator, from a parameter's owner, from a Curator accepting a value BEACON proposed (CLS-05), or from a release that changes a Method.
