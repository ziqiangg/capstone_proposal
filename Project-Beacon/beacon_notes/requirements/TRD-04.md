# TRD-04

IF the evidence does not support a candidate trend, or fewer than [[MIN_TREND_DEVELOPMENTS]] developments constitute it, THEN BEACON shall not assert that trend.

**Rationale.** An asserted trend gets quoted, and a wrong one gets quoted too. The floor is the minimum support that can be tested; what else "the evidence supports" requires is an open question.
