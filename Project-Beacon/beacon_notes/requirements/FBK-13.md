# FBK-13

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to approve Revised results, one at a time or all those of one trigger together.

**Rationale.** The Curator holds product-policy authority, so approving what re-derivation produced is the Curator's decision. One trigger can revise many results, so approving them together keeps the gate usable.
