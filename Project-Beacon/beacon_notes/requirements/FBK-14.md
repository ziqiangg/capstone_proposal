# FBK-14

WHEN a Curator approves a Revised result, BEACON shall show it to Readers in place of the prior result, outside Published Briefings, without marking it as revised.

**Rationale.** The approved result replaces the prior one wherever Readers see it, the development's record included, but never in a past Briefing (FBK-11). A Reader needs the current analysis, not its history; the history is recorded for the non-Reader roles (FBK-18), and EVD-08 keeps the prior version.
