# ASM-06

WHEN an Admitted item or a Revision joins a development, BEACON shall record a new dated assessment of that development.

**Rationale.** A judgement made on the first report of an incident is often wrong by the third. A joining item is forward processing, not re-derivation, so no trigger is needed, and EVD-08 keeps the prior assessment. "Materially new information" cannot be the trigger, because its clause (b) needs this assessment first.
