# SRC-36

BEACON shall put into effect a version of a source's Source Set entry differing from the one in force in an Entry part other than the source's name, or in a Standing, only on a Curator's acceptance of an Onboarding outcome proposing it (SRC-28) or of a version BEACON prepared by reading the source's pages under it, as correcting the one in force (SRC-39).

**Rationale.** A version is judged for drift against what it gave before it was put into effect (A-terms, Drift reference). A version edited by hand gave nothing, so its drift could never be judged and its parts would carry Standings nothing BEACON read gave: one edit would leave a silent hole in the drift tests. A changed part enters as a source does, through an Onboarding (SRC-08), or as a correction BEACON prepared from the source's pages after a Drift flag (SRC-37); either way the version gave something first. The source's name, which nothing judges, stays the Curator's to edit (GOV-17).
