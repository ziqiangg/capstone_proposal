# REL-06

WHEN the scope Method is evaluated against the labelled evaluation set, the proportion of the items that set labels IN to which the Method gives a scope\_decision of OUT shall be at or below [[MAX_MISSED_RATE]].

**Rationale.** A recall floor. Without it, a Method that decides OUT for everything passes REL-04 and REL-05.
