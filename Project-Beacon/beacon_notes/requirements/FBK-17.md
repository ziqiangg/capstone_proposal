# FBK-17

BEACON shall show a Reader no Revised result that a Curator has not approved.

**Rationale.** Re-derived results may be better or worse than the originals, so a person checks each one a Reader could see (an Account, an assessment, a mapping, a trend) before any Reader does. Until then the Reader keeps seeing the prior result, and selection keeps using it (FBK-19).

**Verification.** none yet
