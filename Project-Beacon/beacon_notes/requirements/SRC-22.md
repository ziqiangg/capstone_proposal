# SRC-22

BEACON shall provide a Curator with the means to give BEACON the link to a source, starting an Onboarding of that source, recording the Curator, the time and the reason the Curator gives.

**Rationale.** Whether BEACON should read a source is the Curator's question, since the Curator owns the Source Set, so a Curator is who starts an Onboarding from a link; a Reader who knows of a source tells a Curator, who gives the link. Giving a link adds nothing to the Source Set: it starts the work whose outcome a Curator then accepts or does not (SRC-28). The record says who asked for the source and why, so that the outcome can be read against the request.
