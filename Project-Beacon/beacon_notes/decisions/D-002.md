# D-002 Trigger Register: initial list

## What is decided

The Trigger Register starts with the minimum [[GOV-06]] requires, each trigger with the Judgements it affects:

| Trigger | Judgements it affects |
|---|---|
| A change of a Method's version, a change of model identity included | every Judgement that Method produces |
| A change of the genre vocabulary | item\_type |
| A change of the subject vocabulary | subject tags |
| A change of the Scope Criteria | scope decision |
| A change of the assessment criteria | assessment |
| The activation of a Baseline | the mappings to that Baseline's corpus |
| The Re-extractions that one release, one Curator's acceptance of a corrected Source Set entry or one Operator's command causes ([[ACQ-28]], [[ACQ-29]], [[ACQ-31]]), taken together, where they record a new version of any Article Record ([[ACQ-09]]) | every Judgement on an item whose Article Record that run gave a new version, for items within [[REDERIVE_HORIZON]] |

The last trigger was added by the owner's ruling of 2026-10-05 ("Changed items only"): one run is one trigger, and it re-derives only the Judgements on items whose version it changed, within the horizon; an item older than the horizon gains the new version and has nothing re-derived. A returning item's own Re-extraction ([[ACQ-30]]) is part of the record write of that return, a Stage after its Collection ([[A-terms]], Stage), and is no trigger.

A Curator's correction is not a trigger: it records the corrected value and re-runs nothing ([[FBK-03]]). A merge or a split re-derives the Judgements that rest on membership by its own row ([[DEV-13]]), not through this register.

## What it constrains

Re-derivation of a recorded Judgement happens only on one of these triggers, an Operator's retry, or a merge or split ([[FBK-07]]). The Curator may register further triggers as a change to a Governed Artefact.

## What would reverse it

A Judgement that went stale without any registered trigger firing, found in review, would show a trigger is missing.
