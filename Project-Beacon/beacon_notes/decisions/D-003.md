# D-003 Initial Baselines

## What is decided

BEACON starts with one Baseline per corpus, each active: the SSP as captured on 2026-09-23 ([[CTX-01]]); MITRE ATT&CK 19.2, Enterprise, ICS and Mobile; and MITRE ATLAS 2026.09 ([[CTX-02]]). Each is kept unchanged with its source, capture time and the publisher's version where one exists ([[CTX-12]]).

## What it constrains

Every mapping made before a Curator activates another Baseline ([[CTX-10]]) is made against these.

## What would reverse it

A newer release of a corpus before the first mapping is made; the Curator then activates the newer capture instead ([[CTX-09]], [[CTX-10]]).
