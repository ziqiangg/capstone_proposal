# D-001 Genre vocabulary: initial content

## What is decided

The genre vocabulary ([[A-terms#A3. Genre vocabulary]]) starts with these five values. An item may carry several ([[CLS-01]]); an item that fits none is a vocabulary gap ([[CLS-02]]). Whether an item concerns security at all is a scope decision, never a genre.

| Value | Include | Exclude |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| threats | Adversary capability, technique, tooling or targeting in general terms — a campaign, a TTP, a vulnerability being exploited. Has an actor or a technique. | A named victim with a date (incident); a purely defensive development. |
| capability | A deployable artifact: a tool, product, service, technique, model, or defensive/analytical capability that someone can put into use. New or materially matured. | A finding, analysis or assessment about the world rather than a thing to deploy — that is `research`. |
| research | A finding, analysis, assessment, survey, taxonomy, body of knowledge, or non-authoritative guidance. A peer-reviewed or preprint result. An aggregate report that is predominantly survey data, posture statistics or methodology. | An aggregate report whose content is adversary activity — that is `threats`. A deployable artifact — that is `capability`. |
| regulatory | Law, regulation, directive, mandate, binding advisory, or enforcement action. | A vendor's voluntary framework. |
| incident | A discrete realised event with an identifiable affected party and a time. | Aggregate statistics over many events with no named victim. |

## What it constrains

The first genre classification Method assigns these values and no others. The Curator changes the vocabulary as a Governed Artefact ([[GOV-17]]), and each change is a trigger ([[GOV-06]], [[D-002]]).

## What would reverse it

A share of vocabulary gaps above 10 per cent of Admitted items over a month, or two values that labellers cannot tell apart on a sample, would show the vocabulary needs changing.

## Assumes

The rule that work with no bearing on any security property is `OUT`, not `research`, belongs to the Scope Criteria and waits on [[Q-006]]; until then, [[Q-006]]'s fallback applies it.
